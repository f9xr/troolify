Editorial Policies

How Troolify creates, verifies, and publishes the tools and content on this platform.

Accuracy first

Every tool we publish is tested against known inputs and expected outputs before it goes live. Descriptions state what a tool actually does and never overstate its capabilities. If a bug is reported, we fix it promptly and note the change in the changelog.

Privacy and zero data retention

Troolify tools are designed to run 100% in your browser. Unless a tool explicitly says otherwise, your input never leaves your device and no copy is stored on our servers. We will not add analytics or data-collection to any tool without first updating this policy and the tool's description.

Transparency

We do not accept payment in exchange for featuring a tool or altering its description. "Coming soon" tools are clearly labeled and are not presented as available. Affiliate or sponsored content, if ever added, will always be visibly marked.

Corrections

If an error is identified in any tool or page, contact us at tontufytservices@gmail.com. Corrections are reviewed and deployed within a reasonable timeframe, and significant corrections are logged.

Review process

Content is written by the F9XR editorial team and reviewed before publication. Tools published by the editorial team are reviewed for correctness, accessibility, and clarity against this policy.
