Random Color Generator

The Random Color Generator creates fresh hex colors on demand - a single shade or a whole palette of random colors, each built from random RGB channels rather than a preset list.

How to Use

  1. Set the count - from 1 to 30 colors.
  2. Click Generate random colors.
  3. Copy the hex values from the list.

Why It Matters

Blank-canvas color picking stalls projects. A burst of random candidates unblocks the eye, and since the tool runs locally the palette is genuinely fresh every click - no cached 'random' lists.

Contractor Tip

Generate 8-10 colors, then round them to a shared hue with a palette tool - randomness gives you candidates, structure gives you a design.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are two runs ever identical?

Effectively no - each run draws fresh random channel values, so every click is a new palette.

Conclusion

Spin up random hex colors instantly - one click, fresh palette, fully local.
