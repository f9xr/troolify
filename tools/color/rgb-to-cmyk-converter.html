RGB to CMYK Converter: Prepare Colours for Print

Screens use RGB; printers use CMYK. This RGB to CMYK converter translates between the two so what you design is closer to what gets printed.

Why convert

Printers mix cyan, magenta, yellow and black inks, while screens mix light. Colours can shift, so converting early helps predict the printed result.

Not exact

This is a mathematical conversion, not a colour-managed one. Bright RGB colours may sit outside the CMYK gamut; check proofs for critical work.

How to use RGB to CMYK Converter

  1. Enter red, green and blue values.
  2. Read the CMYK percentages and hex.
  3. Use the swatch to sanity-check.

Tips for getting accurate results

  • Bright greens and blues often shift.
  • Always request a proof.
  • Record CMYK values for reuse.

Key takeaways

  • RGB is additive, CMYK subtractive.
  • Conversion is approximate.
  • Proofs confirm print accuracy.

Frequently asked questions

Is the conversion exact?

No, it is a maths approximation.

What is the K value?

K is black, derived from the darkest channel.

Can CMYK be negative?

Values are clamped to 0-100%.

Is my data uploaded?

No, it stays local.
