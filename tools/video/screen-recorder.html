Screen Recorder

The Screen Recorder captures your screen, a single window or a browser tab using your browser's built-in screen capture - then packages the footage into a downloadable WebM file when you hit stop.

How to Use

  1. Click Start recording and pick what to share (screen, window or tab).
  2. Do your thing - the current tab stays interactive.
  3. Click Stop & download and save the .webm recording.

Why It Matters

A full OBS-style setup is overkill when you just need to capture a corner of the screen for a bug report or a short walkthrough. In-browser capture is instant, plugin-free, and keeps the stream local until you download it.

Contractor Tip

Screen-capture tabs can go blank in some setups - prefer capturing the window or tab you are demonstrating, and keep the page you record in view.

Frequently Asked Questions

What format is the recording saved as?

WebM (Matroska video with a VP8/VP9 codec), the standard output of the browser's MediaRecorder.

Conclusion

Record your screen, window or tab and download the video - instantly, locally and free.
