Video Aspect Ratio Calculator: Right Sizes for Every Platform

Horizontal, vertical, square, cinematic - every video platform has its own shape, and uploading the wrong one means crops, black bars, or letterboxing your carefully shot footage. This calculator works out the missing dimension for any ratio in one step, and tells you which platform presets your ratio matches.

The Ratios You Will Actually Use

  • 16:9 - the widescreen standard for YouTube, Facebook and most web video.
  • 9:16 - vertical video for Shorts, Reels and TikTok - the same relationship, rotated.
  • 1:1 and 4:5 - social media posts that survive Instagram's feed crop.
  • 21:9 and 2.39:1 - ultrawide and cinematic framing.

How to Use the Calculator

  1. Pick a preset - 16:9, 9:16, 4:5, 4:3, 1:1, 21:9 and 2.39:1 are built in, or choose Custom and type any width and height you like.
  2. Say whether you know the width or the height and enter your one known value.
  3. Read the missing dimension - plus the decimal ratio for reference and a warning if the result lands on an odd pixel count.
  4. Scan the table - matching platform resolutions are highlighted automatically.

Camera or Export: Either Way It Works

Know the ratio but not the pixels? Enter a target such as 1080 as the width of a 9:16 clip and the height comes back as 1920 - exactly the Shorts spec. Or feed it a camera's native resolution to see which ratio you are already shooting and where it will fit.

Frequently Asked Questions

What video ratio should I use for YouTube?

16:9 is the standard for horizontal YouTube videos, displayed at 1920 x 1080 (Full HD) or 1280 x 720. Shorts use 9:16 vertical video at 1080 x 1920.

How do I convert 16:9 to a specific size?

Divide your known dimension by the matching side of the ratio. At 16:9, a width of 1280 gives a height of 1280 x (9/16) = 720. The calculator does this instantly for any ratio and any dimension.

What is the difference between 16:9 and 9:16?

16:9 is a landscape (wide) orientation used for standard video, while 9:16 is the exact same relationship rotated into portrait orientation - used for vertical formats like TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

Is the Troolify video aspect ratio calculator free?

Yes. It is completely free, requires no account, and all calculations run locally inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored anywhere.
