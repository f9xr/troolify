Readability Checker: Match Your Audience

Readability scores estimate how hard a text is to read. This readability checker calculates Flesch Reading Ease and the Flesch-Kincaid grade level.

Reading the scores

Reading ease runs from 0 to 100; higher is easier. A score of 60 to 70 suits general audiences. The grade level maps roughly to US school years.

Improving readability

Shorten sentences, prefer common words, and break up long paragraphs. Aim for the level your readers expect.

How to use Readability Checker

  1. Paste your text.
  2. Press Check readability.
  3. Read the ease and grade scores.
