Multiline Slug Generator: Batch-Clean Your URLs in One Paste

Every page you publish needs a URL that is short, readable and descriptive. When you are rolling out a content calendar - ten new posts, fifty product pages, a site migration - converting each title by hand is slow and error-prone. This tool turns a whole list into clean slugs in one pass.

What a Slug Actually Is

A slug is the part of the URL after the domain that names a page: multiline-slug-generator in this very address. Good slugs are lowercase, hyphens between words, punctuation stripped, and short enough to be read - and shared - at a glance.

Why Clean Slugs Matter

  • Readability - humans scan URLs, and a readable URL has higher click-through.
  • Search parsing - Google treats hyphens as word separators, so best-coffee-beans is understood as three relevant words.
  • Sharing - an ugly, padded slug looks broken in a message or a printed QR code.

How Keyword Optimization Works

Turn on Keyword optimization and the tool scans every line, counting how often each word appears across your whole list. It then keeps, in order, the highest-frequency words that each line contains - so the terms your audience actually repeats become the skeleton of the slug. The top keywords appear as chips under the results, ready to inspire titles too.

A Fast Batch Workflow

  1. Paste your titles - one per line.
  2. Switch on "Remove stop words" to drop the, of, to and similar filler that adds nothing to a URL.
  3. Cap the length - 50-60 characters is a comfortable ceiling.
  4. Enable keyword optimization if you want slug suggestions biased toward the words that repeat most.
  5. Copy all slugs - or grab each one individually with its copy button - and paste straight into your CMS.

Check the resulting slugs side by side with the Meta Description Checker so the title and URL agree on the same keyword.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a URL slug?

The readable part of a URL that names a page, like "multiline-slug-generator" here. Clean slugs use lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens, drop punctuation, and stay short.

How long should a URL slug be?

Under roughly 50-60 characters. Short descriptive slugs display fully in results, share cleanly, and concentrate keyword value in fewer words.

Hyphens or underscores in slugs?

Hyphens. Search engines read hyphens as word separators but treat underscores as joining characters, so "best-coffee-beans" parses as three words.

Do keywords in a URL help rankings?

Marginally. Slug keywords are a small on-page signal and a click-through boost. Content, relevance and authority do the heavy lifting - the slug just closes the deal.

Conclusion

Batch slugging turns an hour of tedious, inconsistent URL surgery into a thirty-second paste. Clean URLs, consistent formatting, and slugs that carry your most important keywords - every project should start that way.
