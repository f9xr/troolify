Open Graph Generator: Control Social Previews

When someone shares your link, Open Graph tags decide the title, description and image shown. This Open Graph generator builds them along with Twitter Card tags.

Key tags

og:title, og:description, og:image and og:url do most of the work. Twitter cards mirror them for X.

Image guidance

Use a landscape image around 1200x630 pixels with a 1.91:1 ratio. Absolute URLs are essential.

How to use Open Graph Generator

  1. Fill in title, description, URL and image.
  2. Choose a Twitter card type.
  3. Paste the tags into your head.
