Meta Description Checker: Stop Writing Titles Google Truncates

Google only shows a limited window of your title and description before cutting them off with an ellipsis. Titles that spill past roughly 600 pixels, or descriptions over about 160 characters, get truncated - and truncated snippets tend to earn fewer clicks. This checker tells you instantly how close you are to the edge, as you type.

What the Checker Lets You See

  • Character count for both your title tag and meta description.
  • Pocket-friendly progress bars against the recommended limits (~60 characters for titles, ~160 for descriptions).
  • Pixel-width estimate - a lightweight approximation of how wide your text will render on a desktop result page.
  • A live Google-style preview of how your snippet looks in search results.

The Recommended Lengths Explained

The rule of thumb is about 50-60 characters for titles and 120-160 characters for descriptions. The real limit is pixel width - the same text can truncate on a narrow mobile screen sooner than on desktop. This tool combines both character and pixel estimates so you can aim for a length that survives both.

How to Use It

  1. Paste your title into the title field and check the count, bar and state label.
  2. Add your description and repeat - the tool flags good, warning, and too-long states.
  3. Read the snippet preview as you revise, until the full title and description display cleanly.
  4. Keep tweaking until the bars sit inside the safe zone and the snippet reads naturally.

Writing Titles and Descriptions That Perform

Put your most important keyword and your value proposition in the first 40-50 characters of the title, since that is the part that almost always displays. For the description, front-load the benefit, include a call to action, and avoid clickbait that makes you lose clicks when the snippet delivers. Pair this tool with the Schema Markup Generator to also mark up your content for rich results, and use the Text to Slug Converter to tidy the URL that appears under your title.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the ideal title tag length?

About 50 to 60 characters is a safe target for title tags. Google truncates long titles in search results, but the exact cut-off depends on pixel width, so shorter titles that fit within roughly 600 pixels stay fully visible.

What is the ideal meta description length?

Aim for 120 to 160 characters. Google typically shows around 155 to 160 characters of the description in search results and will cut off anything longer with an ellipsis.

Is the Troolify SEO checker accurate?

The tool gives a reliable estimate using character counts plus a lightweight pixel-width approximation for common desktop result widths. Google's exact rendering varies by device and font, so treat it as a strong guideline rather than an exact measurement.

Does a longer meta description improve rankings?

Not directly. There is no ranking boost from longer descriptions, but a well-written description within the displayable length improves click-through rates, which can indirectly help your pages perform better in search.

Conclusion

Search snippets are your page's first impression. A few characters of trimming can be the difference between a title that reads perfectly and one that trails off mid-word. Run every title and description through this checker before you publish.
