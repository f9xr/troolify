Convert Word to PDF: Fast, Private, and Free

Need to convert a Word document to PDF without uploading it to a third-party server? This browser-based tool reads your .docx file (or pasted text) and generates a clean, downloadable PDF entirely on your machine. No account, no upload, no data retention.

About the Tool

Under the hood, the tool uses JSZip to unpack the .docx archive and extract the XML-based text content. It walks the document's paragraph nodes, collects the plain text, and then feeds it into pdf-lib to lay out each line on a US Letter page using Helvetica 11pt font. Text lines are automatically wrapped to fit the page width.

If your source is a legacy .doc file or any plain text, you can paste it directly into the textarea instead. The same PDF rendering pipeline applies.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Upload or paste — choose a .docx file via the file picker, or paste your text into the textarea.
  2. Click Convert — the tool reads the file and builds a PDF in seconds.
  3. Download — click the download link to save the generated PDF to your device.

Honest Limitations

This tool is designed for text-based conversion. Complex formatting such as tables, images, headers/footers, charts, and embedded objects are not extracted. The output is a clean, readable PDF with consistent typography. For full-fidelity conversion of heavily formatted documents, desktop applications like Microsoft Word or LibreOffice Writer are recommended.

Why It Matters

Many online Word-to-PDF converters require you to upload your document to their servers, raising concerns about confidential business proposals, legal contracts, or personal resumes. With this tool, your data never leaves the browser tab — ideal for sensitive documents that should stay private.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this tool preserve Word formatting like bold, italic and tables?

This tool extracts plain text from your Word document and renders it as a clean PDF. Complex formatting such as tables, images, headers/footers, and embedded objects are not carried over. For full-fidelity conversion, desktop software like Microsoft Word or LibreOffice may be more suitable.

What file types are supported?

You can upload .docx files, or paste plain text directly into the textarea. Legacy .doc and .rtf files are not parsed as Word archives, but their text content can be pasted into the textarea for conversion.

Is my document uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens inside your browser using JavaScript. Your file never leaves your device - no uploads, no servers, zero data retention.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Like all Troolify tools this runs on desktop and mobile browsers with no install and no account required.

Conclusion

Whether you need to share a resume as PDF or convert meeting notes into a portable format, this tool gives you a fast, private alternative to uploading your documents to unknown servers. Try it next to the Text to PDF Converter or the Images to PDF tool for different source formats.
