Remove Pages from PDF: Trim Your Document Without Uploading It

Whether you are cutting a cover letter, dropping appendix pages, or keeping only the section a client asked for, removing pages from a PDF should be quick and painless. This tool reads and rewrites the file entirely in your browser with the pdf-lib library.

About the Tool

Select pages to delete using simple ranges like 2,5,7-10, or click the interactive page chips below the input - each chip toggles whether that page is marked for removal. Need the opposite? Flip "Show pages to KEEP instead" to invert the selection and keep only the pages you chose.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Choose a PDF with the file picker - the total page count appears instantly.
  2. Select pages to remove either by typing ranges or clicking the numbered chips.
  3. Optionally invert with the "keep instead" checkbox to keep only your selection.
  4. Press Remove Pages, then download your trimmed PDF.

Why It Matters

Deleting pages on your own device keeps sensitive documents away from cloud uploaders and paid services. The trimmed file keeps the same content and fonts because pages are carried over by reference, not re-rendered - giving a compact result that preserves the original quality.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I remove multiple non-consecutive pages at once?

Yes. Type ranges like 2,5,7-10 or click individual page chips in the grid. Page numbers are 1-based, and any number of selections is supported.

What does 'Show pages to KEEP instead' do?

It inverts your selection: instead of removing the selected pages, the tool removes everything EXCEPT them. This is useful to keep just a few pages out of a long document.

Is the Troolify version safe for my data?

Yes. Everything runs locally inside your browser tab - nothing you paste or upload is sent to a server, so drafts and data stay private.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Like all Troolify tools this runs on desktop and mobile browsers with no install and no account required.
