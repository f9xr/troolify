Convert seconds, minutes, hours, days, weeks, months and years using common time constants.

How to use

  1. Enter the amount you want to convert.
  2. Pick the source and target units from the dropdowns.
  3. Press Convert to see the summary tiles.
  4. Switch unit families using the category link above the converter.
