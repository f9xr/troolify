Fake Email Generator: Synthetic Test Email Addresses on Demand

QA engineers, developers and data engineers need large batches of realistic-looking email addresses for form testing, database seeding and user flow validation. The Troolify Fake Email Generator creates synthetic emails across public and dev-safe domains - all generated locally in your browser.

What the Fake Email Generator Does

The tool combines first names, last names, numeric suffixes and domain names to produce unique-looking email addresses. You can choose public domains like Gmail and Outlook, dev-safe domains like example.com and test.com, or a mix of both. Optional full name and domain fields let you control the output. No data is uploaded anywhere.

How to Use the Fake Email Generator

  1. Set the count - enter how many emails to generate (1-100).
  2. Choose domain type - All mixed, Public only or Dev-safe only.
  3. Toggle optional fields - enable full name and domain output as needed.
  4. Choose export format - Table, CSV, JSON or Plain text.
  5. Copy or download - grab the output instantly.

Use Cases

QA teams seed test databases with bulk email addresses. Developers validate email input fields and regex patterns. Marketing platforms test newsletter signup flows. Auth systems test password reset and verification email workflows with synthetic addresses.

Frequently Asked Questions

What domain types are supported?

The generator supports three modes: All mixed (combines public and dev-safe), Public only (gmail.com, outlook.com, yahoo.com, hotmail.com) and Dev-safe only (example.com, test.com, mailinator.com, guerrillamail.com).

What does 'dev-safe' mean?

Dev-safe domains are addresses specifically designated for testing and documentation (like example.com and test.com) or disposable email services. They are safe to use in code samples and test environments.

Is my data safe?

100% safe. All email generation happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing is uploaded or stored on any server.

Conclusion

Whether you are testing a signup form, seeding a test database or validating email regex patterns, the Troolify Fake Email Generator gives you synthetic email addresses in seconds, entirely in your browser.
