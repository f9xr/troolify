Random Address Generator: Test Addresses on Demand

Software testers, QA engineers and developers need realistic addresses to validate forms, seed databases and test shipping integrations. The Troolify Random Address Generator creates fully-formed fake addresses across 15 countries, with correct postcode formats, state codes and phone dialling rules, all generated locally in your browser.

What the Random Address Generator Does

The tool pairs each country with its own city list, region codes and postcode rules, so a German address gets a 5-digit postcode and a UK address gets a valid outward and inward code. Phone numbers use the right country dialling code and national length, and the recipient email and phone stay fixed when you switch output formats. You choose the country, the count and optional fields like recipient name, email and phone number. No data is uploaded anywhere.

How to Use the Random Address Generator

  1. Pick a country - choose one of the 15 supported countries in the dropdown.
  2. Set the count - enter how many addresses to generate (1-100).
  3. Toggle optional fields - enable names, emails and phone numbers as needed.
  4. Choose export format - Table, CSV, JSON, SQL, Playwright or Cypress.
  5. Copy or download - grab the output instantly.

Use Cases

E-commerce developers test checkout forms with country-specific postcode formats. QA teams seed test databases with realistic address rows. Mobile developers validate address autocomplete integrations. Data engineers use bulk addresses for ETL pipeline tests.

Most forms want a person attached to the address. The fake detail generator pairs an address with a name, email, phone and date of birth in one record. Check a batch with the CSV validator before it reaches a fixture file, then browse the tools catalog.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which countries are supported?

15 countries: the US, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany, France, India, Brazil, Japan, Mexico, Spain, Italy, the Netherlands, Sweden and Singapore. Each one has its own city list, state or province codes, postcode format and phone dialling rules.

Are the generated addresses real?

They are synthetic. Street and city names are real words and the postcode, state and phone formats are correct for the country, so validators accept them, but no address resolves to a real household and nothing is deliverable.

Can I export addresses to Playwright or Cypress?

Yes. The Playwright export is a runnable data array and the Cypress export is a fixture block. Table, CSV, JSON and SQL INSERT statements are there too. The name, email and phone switches apply to all of them.

Is my data safe?

Generation runs in your browser with JavaScript. Nothing is uploaded, logged or stored on a server.

Conclusion

Whether you are testing a checkout flow, seeding a test database or validating a geocoding API, the Troolify Random Address Generator gives you fake addresses from 15 countries in seconds, entirely in your browser.
