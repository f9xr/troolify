Fake Detail Generator: Synthetic Person Profiles on Demand

QA engineers and developers need realistic person records to seed test databases, populate forms and build fixtures for front-end and E2E tests. The Troolify Fake Detail Generator creates complete synthetic profiles with name, email, phone, address, date of birth and company, all generated locally in your browser.

What the Fake Detail Generator Does

The tool randomly combines first names, last names, email patterns, phone numbers, addresses, birth dates and company names into realistic profiles. The date of birth is drawn between 18 and 79 years ago and the age field is computed from it, so the two never disagree. Address formats adapt to the selected country (US, UK, CA or AU) so fields match local conventions, and the date format changes with it. No data is uploaded anywhere.

How to Use the Fake Detail Generator

  1. Pick a country - choose US, UK, Canada or Australia.
  2. Set the count - enter how many profiles to generate (1-50).
  3. Toggle optional fields - enable email, phone, address, DOB and company as needed.
  4. Choose export format - Table, JSON, CSV, SQL, Playwright or Cypress.
  5. Copy or download - grab the output instantly.

Use Cases

QA teams seed authentication databases with test users. E2E test engineers build Playwright and Cypress fixtures. CRM developers test address autocomplete and profile forms. Data engineers use bulk profiles for ETL pipeline validation.

If your form validates an address on its own, pull a separate list from the random address generator. For account fixtures that also need a mailbox, the fake email generator covers public, reserved and disposable domains. The tools catalog lists everything else.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which countries are supported?

The US, UK, Canada and Australia. The date of birth is formatted for the country (MM/DD/YYYY, DD/MM/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD), postcodes follow the local pattern, and phone numbers use the country dialling code.

What fields are included in a fake profile?

A name is always included. Email, phone, address, date of birth with a matching age, and company are switches you can turn on, and every switch applies to the table and to all six exports.

Are the generated people real?

No. Names, emails, phone numbers and addresses are combined from lists, and the date of birth is drawn between 18 and 79 years ago, so no real person's record is reproduced.

What export formats are available?

Table, JSON, CSV, SQL INSERT statements, a Playwright data array and a Cypress fixture block.

Conclusion

Whether you are seeding a test database, building E2E fixtures or validating profile forms, the Troolify Fake Detail Generator gives you synthetic person profiles in seconds, entirely in your browser.
