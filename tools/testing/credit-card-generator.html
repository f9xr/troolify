Credit Card Generator: Luhn-Valid Test Card Numbers on Demand

QA engineers and front-end developers need realistic test card numbers to validate payment forms, checkout flows and subscription pages. The Troolify Credit Card Generator creates Luhn-valid card numbers for Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover and JCB - all generated locally in your browser with zero server contact.

What the Credit Card Generator Does

The tool builds card numbers that pass the Luhn algorithm (mod-10 check), making them valid for front-end form validation and sandbox testing. It supports the standard BIN ranges for five major card networks and optionally adds future expiry dates and random CVV codes. No data is uploaded anywhere.

How to Use the Credit Card Generator

  1. Pick a card type - choose Visa, MasterCard, Amex, Discover, JCB or Random.
  2. Set the count - enter how many card numbers to generate (1-50).
  3. Toggle optional fields - enable expiry dates and CVV as needed.
  4. Choose export format - Table, CSV, JSON or Playwright.
  5. Copy or download - grab the output instantly.

Use Cases

E-commerce developers validate Stripe or PayPal sandbox checkout forms. Mobile developers test in-app purchase flows. QA engineers run automated regression tests on payment pages. Subscription platforms use generated cards to test recurring billing logic.

Frequently Asked Questions

What card types are supported?

The generator supports Visa (starts with 4, 16 digits), MasterCard (starts with 51-55 or 2221-2720, 16 digits), American Express (starts with 34 or 37, 15 digits), Discover (starts with 6011 or 65, 16 digits) and JCB (starts with 3528-3589, 15-16 digits). You can also pick Random to mix all types.

Are the generated card numbers valid?

All generated card numbers pass the Luhn algorithm (mod 10 check). This means they will pass basic front-end validation but they are not real card numbers and cannot be used for actual transactions.

Is my data safe?

100% safe. All credit card number generation happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing is uploaded or stored on any server.

Conclusion

Whether you are testing a checkout flow, validating a payment form or running automated QA on billing pages, the Troolify Credit Card Generator gives you Luhn-valid test card numbers in seconds, entirely in your browser.
