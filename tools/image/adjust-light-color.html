Light & Color Adjuster: Fix Photo Lighting in Your Browser

Not every photo comes out perfectly exposed. Shadows may be too dark, highlights blown out, or the white balance just feels off. The Troolify Light & Color Adjuster lets you fix all of that with eight intuitive sliders — exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, whites, blacks, temperature and tint — and see the result in real time before downloading. Everything happens locally in your browser, so your original photos are never uploaded to any server.

What the Light & Color Adjuster Does

Upload any image and the tool reads every pixel on your device. You then move sliders to shift brightness in the highlights, midtones and shadows independently, push or pull contrast, and warm or cool the color balance. Because each adjustment is applied directly to the pixel data on a hidden canvas, you see the effect instantly as a live preview — no server round-trips, no waiting.

The tool supports common adjustments found in desktop photo editors: Exposure shifts overall brightness; Contrast widens or narrows the tonal range; Highlights and Shadows target the bright and dark extremes; Whites and Blacks set the clipping points; Temperature shifts the image between warm (yellow/orange) and cool (blue); and Tint balances between green and magenta.

How to Use the Light & Color Adjuster

  1. Upload an image — click the drop zone or drag a PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF or BMP onto it.
  2. Adjust sliders — move any of the eight sliders and watch the preview update instantly.
  3. Compare — toggle the Before / After checkbox to flip between the original and adjusted version.
  4. Pick an export format — choose PNG for lossless quality or JPEG for a smaller file size.
  5. Download — click the Download button to save the adjusted image to your device.

Tips for Better Photo Adjustments

  • Start with exposure — get the overall brightness right before fine-tuning the other sliders.
  • Use highlights and shadows separately — this gives you more control than just pushing the exposure slider.
  • Temperature first, then tint — correct the warm/cool balance before adjusting the green/magenta axis.
  • Check on a calibrated screen — monitor brightness and color settings affect how adjustments look.
  • Download as PNG for further editing — if you plan to adjust again, keep the data loss-free.

Frequently Asked Questions

What adjustments can I make with this tool?

You can adjust exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, whites, blacks, color temperature and tint. Each slider gives you real-time control so you can fine-tune the look of your image before downloading.

Is the Troolify light and color adjuster safe to use?

Yes. The image is loaded and processed entirely inside your browser using the HTML Canvas API. No file is ever uploaded to any server, so your photos remain completely private.

What image formats does the adjuster support?

Any image your browser can decode, including PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF and BMP. The adjusted image can be downloaded as a PNG or JPEG.

Can I reset all adjustments back to default?

Yes. Click the Reset button to set every slider back to its neutral default value. You can also click Clear to remove the uploaded image and start over.
