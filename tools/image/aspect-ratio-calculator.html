Aspect Ratio Calculator: Every Resolution, Instant Result

Before you export a thumbnail, crop a photo, or render a video, you need to know one thing above all: the ratio. Get it wrong and platforms stretch, chop, or letterbox your work. This calculator simplifies any width and height to its true ratio and then finds the matching resolution you need.

What Is an Aspect Ratio, Really?

The aspect ratio is the proportional relationship between an image's width and its height, written as two numbers like 16:9. It tells you the shape, not the size. A 1920×1080 video and a 1280×720 video are the same 16:9 ratio, just rendered at different resolutions. The decimal form, for example 1.78, is the width divided by the height.

How to Use the Calculator

  1. Pick a preset - 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 9:16, 21:9 and 2:1 are pre-loaded, or choose Custom.
  2. Check the numbers - the width and height boxes fill automatically, and the GCD simplifies the ratio for you.
  3. Enter a target dimension - tell the tool whether you know the width or the height.
  4. Read the missing dimension - the second number is computed instantly, e.g. width 1280 at 16:9 gives height 720.

Common Ratios and Where They're Used

  • 16:9 - YouTube, widescreen monitors, HD broadcasting.
  • 9:16 - TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts (vertical video).
  • 4:3 - classic photography, older displays, document scans.
  • 1:1 - square profile pictures and product grids.
  • 21:9 and 2.39:1 - ultrawide and cinematic formats.

Putting It to Work

Designers use the ratio to size social media art and thumbnails, video editors use it to match delivery specs, and developers use it to reserve layout space without distortion. Once you know the ratio, resize actual photos to it with the Image Resizer, or embed a version into your code with the Image to Base64 Converter.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate the aspect ratio of an image?

Divide the width by the height to get the decimal ratio, then simplify both numbers by their greatest common divisor. For example 1920 x 1080 becomes 16:9 because both divide cleanly by 120.

What does 16:9 aspect ratio mean?

16:9 means that for every 16 units of width there are 9 units of height. It is the standard widescreen format used by HD and 4K video, most widescreen monitors, and YouTube content.

How do I get the missing dimension for a target resolution?

Multiply the known dimension by the other side of the ratio. If you know the width, the height equals width x (ratio height / ratio width). The calculator does this instantly for any target value you enter.
