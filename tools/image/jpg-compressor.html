JPG Compressor: Shrink Photo File Sizes Without Leaving Your Browser

Large JPEG photos slow down websites, eat into upload limits and waste storage. The Troolify JPG Compressor reduces file size in seconds with a single quality slider - no software to install, no server upload, no risk to your privacy.

What the JPG Compressor Does

Upload any JPG or JPEG image and the tool decodes it locally using the browser canvas API. Move the quality slider between 10 and 100 and the image is re-encoded at that quality level in real time. A live comparison shows the original next to the compressed version so you can see exactly how much detail is retained.

The results card breaks down the original file size, compressed file size and the percentage saved. Because every step happens inside your browser, your photos are never sent to a server.

How to Use the JPG Compressor

  1. Upload a JPG - click the drop zone or drag a JPEG file onto it.
  2. Adjust the quality slider - lower values mean smaller files; higher values keep more detail.
  3. Compare before and after - preview both versions side by side to find the sweet spot.
  4. Check the savings - review the file size difference and percentage saved.
  5. Download - save the compressed JPG to your device.

Tips for Effective JPG Compression

  • Quality 70-85 is the sweet spot - most photographs look great at these values with significant file size reduction.
  • Start high, lower gradually - begin at 80 and decrease until you notice artifacts, then bump it back up slightly.
  • Compress once - re-saving a JPG multiple times compounds quality loss, so aim to compress your original at the right level in one pass.
  • Pair with resizing - if the image dimensions are also too large, use the Image Resizer first to scale down, then compress.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does JPG compression work?

JPG compression uses a lossy algorithm that removes subtle color and detail information that the human eye is less likely to notice. Lower quality values produce smaller files but may introduce visible artifacts. Higher values retain more detail at the cost of a larger file.

Is the Troolify JPG compressor safe to use?

Yes. The image is loaded and compressed entirely inside your browser using the HTML canvas API. Your photo is never uploaded to any server, so it stays completely private on your device.

What quality setting should I use for JPG compression?

A quality setting between 70 and 85 offers a good balance between file size and visual quality for most photographs. Settings below 50 will show noticeable artifacts. Use the live preview to find the sweet spot for your specific image.

Can I compress a JPG multiple times without extra quality loss?

No. Each time a JPG is re-saved with lossy compression, additional detail is removed. This is called generation loss. For the best results, compress your original image once at the desired quality level.
