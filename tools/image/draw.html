Drawing Canvas: Freehand Painting Right in Your Browser

Sometimes you need to sketch an idea, touch up a screenshot, annotate a diagram or just doodle for fun without installing heavy software. The Troolify Drawing Canvas gives you a full-featured painting surface with eight brush styles, a flood-fill bucket, an eyedropper for colour sampling, adjustable size and opacity, and a File menu to open existing images, start a fresh canvas or export your work as PNG or JPEG.

What the Drawing Canvas Does

The tool renders an HTML5 canvas element and tracks pointer events in real time. Choose from Round, Square, Calligraphy, Spray, Crayon, Marker, or Pattern brush tips, adjust the diameter and opacity, then paint freely. Switch to the Paint Bucket to flood-fill contiguous colour regions, or use the Eyedropper to sample any on-screen pixel and load it into the colour picker.

Everything runs locally. No brush strokes, fills or images ever leave your browser tab. When you are satisfied, export the canvas as a lossless PNG or a compact JPEG with one click.

How to Use the Drawing Canvas

  1. Pick a brush style - choose Round, Square, Calligraphy, Spray, Crayon, Marker or Pattern from the dropdown.
  2. Adjust size and opacity - drag the sliders to set brush diameter and transparency.
  3. Choose a colour - click the colour swatch to pick any hue, or use the Eyedropper to sample from the canvas.
  4. Draw - click and drag on the canvas to paint. Use the Paint Bucket to fill enclosed regions.
  5. Undo / Redo - press Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y to navigate history.
  6. Export - open the File menu and choose Export as PNG or JPEG to save your work.

Tips for Better Paintings

  • Lower opacity for blending - set opacity to 40-60% and layer strokes to create soft gradients and colour mixing.
  • Use the Crayon brush for texture - its rough edges add a hand-drawn feel to quick sketches.
  • Calligraphy for lettering - the angled flat tip mimics a chisel nib, perfect for headings and labels.
  • Spray for shading - the scattered-dot pattern creates an airbrush effect at low opacity.
  • Keyboard shortcuts speed you up - B for brush, E for eraser, G for fill, I for eyedropper, [ / ] to resize.

Frequently Asked Questions

What brush styles does the Drawing Canvas support?

The Drawing Canvas includes eight brush styles: Round (soft circle), Square (hard-edged), Calligraphy (angled flat tip), Spray (airbrush scatter), Crayon (textured wax), Marker (broad semi-transparent stroke), Eraser (removes pixels), and Pattern (checkerboard stamp). Each responds to brush size and opacity settings.

How does the flood-fill (paint bucket) tool work?

Click any pixel with the paint bucket selected and the tool performs a breadth-first fill of all contiguous pixels that match the clicked colour within a small tolerance. The entire connected region is replaced with your currently selected colour.

Can I pick a colour from the canvas itself?

Yes. Select the eyedropper tool and click anywhere on the canvas. The tool reads the pixel colour at that exact point using getImageData and sets it as your active drawing colour.

Is my drawing data uploaded to a server?

No. Every brush stroke, fill operation and colour pick happens entirely inside your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. No image data leaves your device.
