Image Cropper: Cut Down to the Exact Pixels You Need

Not every photo is perfectly framed. A distracting edge, an unwanted watermark in the corner, or a social-media aspect ratio requirement can all be solved with a quick crop. The Troolify Image Cropper lets you draw a rectangle directly on your image - by dragging a visual overlay or by entering exact pixel coordinates - and export the cropped result as a high-quality PNG or JPEG, entirely in your browser.

What the Image Cropper Does

Upload any image and it is decoded locally on the canvas. A semi-transparent overlay dims the area outside your crop rectangle. Drag the box to reposition it, grab any of the eight handles to resize it, or type precise width, height, X and Y values into the input fields. Toggle Lock aspect ratio to constrain the crop box to a fixed proportion while you resize. The result updates live so you always see exactly what you will get.

Because every pixel stays in your browser tab, sensitive documents, ID scans and personal photos are never sent anywhere.

How to Use the Image Cropper

  1. Upload an image - click the drop zone or drag a PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF onto it.
  2. Position the crop box - drag the rectangle to the area you want to keep.
  3. Resize the crop box - pull any corner or edge handle, or type exact dimensions in the fields.
  4. Preview - the cropped result is shown live below the canvas.
  5. Download - pick PNG or JPEG, adjust quality, and save the cropped image.

Tips for Clean Crops

  • Use exact pixels for social media - Instagram square posts need 1080×1080, stories need 1080×1920.
  • Lock the ratio for thumbnails - toggle aspect-ratio lock to keep a 16:9 or 4:3 frame while you reposition.
  • Check the offset fields - if you need the crop to start at a precise pixel, enter X and Y directly.
  • Export PNG for graphics, JPEG for photographs, and use the quality slider for smaller files.

Common Use Cases

Bloggers crop screenshots to remove browser chrome. Product sellers trim whitespace around items. Social media managers reformat landscape photos into portrait crops for stories. Job seekers trim passport scans to fit application forms. Need to resize after cropping? Use our Image Resizer for the final dimensions. To fix a sideways crop instead, use the Image Rotator, or clean up the background with the Background Remover. After cropping a batch of shots, arrange them into a polished Photo Album.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I crop an image to exact dimensions?

Upload your image, then enter the desired width and height in pixels in the crop fields. The visual crop box on the canvas will resize to match, and you can drag it to the exact area you want. The result is previewed live before you download.

Is the Troolify image cropper safe to use?

Yes. The image is loaded and processed entirely inside your browser using the HTML canvas API. No file is ever uploaded to any server, so your photos stay completely private.

What image formats can I crop?

Any image format your browser can decode, including PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF and BMP. The cropped result can be exported as a PNG or a JPEG.

Can I drag the crop area visually?

Yes. The tool renders your image on a canvas and overlays a crop rectangle that you can drag to reposition and resize using the corner and edge handles. The numeric fields stay in sync as you move the box.

Conclusion

Cropping images used to require a desktop photo editor. The Troolify Image Cropper does it in seconds, right in your browser, with full visual control and exact pixel input - perfect for getting every image just right before you publish.
