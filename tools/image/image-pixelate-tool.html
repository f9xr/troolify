Image Pixelate Tool: Mosaic and Retro Effects

Pixelation turns an image into blocks. This image pixelate tool downscales then upscales your photo for a mosaic effect you control.

How pixelation works

The image is drawn to a smaller canvas, averaging blocks of colour, then stretched back with smoothing off. Larger blocks mean a chunkier mosaic.

Retro and privacy uses

Pixelation gives a deliberate retro or censor-bar look. For privacy it must be coarse enough that detail cannot be recovered.

How to use Image Pixelate Tool

  1. Choose an image.
  2. Adjust the block size.
  3. Download the PNG.
