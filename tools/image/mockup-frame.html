Mockup Frame: Make Your Screenshots Look Professional, Instantly

A bare screenshot looks fine to you but dull to everyone else. Wrapping it in a device frame - a phone bezel, a browser chrome, a laptop outline - instantly signals context and polish. The Troolify Mockup Frame tool does this entirely in your browser: upload a screenshot, pick a frame type, add a heading and a background, optionally tilt the result in 3D, then download the final PNG.

What the Mockup Frame Tool Does

Choose from five device frames: browser (with address bar), phone (portrait bezel), tablet (landscape bezel), laptop (with keyboard base), and desktop monitor (with stand). Each frame is drawn around your screenshot using the HTML canvas API. You can set a gradient, solid colour, or pattern background, overlay a heading, apply drop shadows, and add a 3D perspective tilt.

Because all rendering happens in the browser, your screenshot never leaves your device. No uploads, no servers, no privacy risk.

How to Use the Mockup Frame

  1. Upload a screenshot - click the drop zone and pick a PNG, JPEG, WebP, or GIF from your device.
  2. Pick a frame type - choose from browser, phone, tablet, laptop, or monitor.
  3. Choose a background - set a gradient, solid colour, or pattern behind the device.
  4. Add a heading - type a title and set the font size and colour.
  5. Adjust tilt & shadow - use the sliders for 3D perspective and drop shadow intensity.
  6. Download - click Generate to preview, then Download PNG to save the result.

Tips for Great Mockups

  • Use high-res screenshots - the device frame is drawn on top, so a crisp screenshot makes a crisp mockup.
  • Keep the heading short - one or two words look best above a device frame.
  • Subtle tilt, big impact - a 5-10 degree tilt adds depth without looking overdone.
  • Match the background - use a dark gradient for a sleek look or a light pattern for a playful feel.
  • Trim whitespace - toggle the trim option to auto-crop excess padding from your screenshot.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a mockup frame and why should I use one?

A mockup frame is a device-shaped border - like a browser window, phone bezel, or laptop outline - that wraps around a screenshot. It makes your screenshots look polished and professional for presentations, blog posts, app store listings, and social media without needing a design tool.

Is the Troolify mockup frame tool safe to use?

Yes. Your screenshot is loaded and rendered entirely inside your browser using the HTML canvas API. Nothing is uploaded to any server, so your images stay completely private.

Can I add a heading or title above my mockup?

Yes. The tool lets you type a custom heading that is rendered above the device frame. You can choose the font size and colour to match your branding.

What frame types are available?

You can choose from five frame types: browser window (with address bar), phone (portrait bezel), tablet (landscape bezel), laptop (with keyboard base), and desktop monitor (with stand). Each is drawn around your screenshot automatically.

Conclusion

Adding a device frame to a screenshot used to require photo editing software and a template. The Troolify Mockup Frame tool does it in seconds, entirely in your browser, with full control over the frame type, background, heading, shadows and 3D tilt.
