Photo Album Maker: Build Print-Ready Albums in Your Browser

Whether you are preserving holiday memories, creating a wedding album or assembling a portfolio, the Troolify Photo Album Maker turns loose photos into a polished, print-ready PDF. Upload your images, pick a layout, add a cover title and preview every page before exporting. Everything runs in your browser so your photos never leave your device.

What the Photo Album Maker Does

After uploading one or more photos you can arrange them into an album with a customizable cover page. Choose from three page layouts - 1 photo per page for full-bleed images, 2 photos per page for side-by-side pairings, or 4 photos per page for a grid view. Drag thumbnails to reorder pages and the preview updates instantly.

The album can be exported as a PDF ready for printing at home or sending to a print shop. Because all rendering happens on the HTML canvas API, your photos are completely private.

How to Use the Photo Album Maker

  1. Upload photos - click the drop zone and select multiple images (PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF).
  2. Enter a cover title - give your album a name and optional subtitle.
  3. Pick a layout - choose 1, 2 or 4 photos per page.
  4. Drag to reorder - rearrange the thumbnails to set page order.
  5. Preview - scroll through the rendered album pages.
  6. Export PDF - download a print-ready PDF to your device.

Tips for a Great Photo Album

  • Use high-resolution source photos - the PDF quality depends on the input images.
  • Mix layouts - start with a 4-photo grid for an overview, then switch to 1-per-page for hero shots.
  • Add a subtitle - a date range or location makes the cover page feel more personal.
  • Match the page size to your printer - A4 for international and Letter for US printers.
  • Lower the quality slider for smaller file sizes when emailing the PDF.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a photo album maker tool?

A photo album maker lets you arrange multiple photos into page layouts, add a cover page with a title and export the result as a print-ready PDF. The Troolify version runs entirely in your browser with no uploads required.

Can I choose different page layouts for my album?

Yes. You can pick from three layouts: 1 photo per page for full-bleed shots, 2 photos per page for side-by-side comparisons and 4 photos per page for a grid. You can change the layout at any time and the album updates instantly.

Is the photo album maker free and private?

Yes. The tool is completely free and processes everything inside your browser. Your photos are never uploaded to any server, so they stay fully private.

How do I reorder pages in my album?

After uploading photos you can drag and drop any thumbnail to rearrange pages. The cover is always page one and can be edited with the controls provided.
