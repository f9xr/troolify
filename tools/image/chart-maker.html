Chart & Graph Maker: Visualise Your Data

Numbers speak louder as pictures. This chart maker turns labelled values into bar, line or pie charts you can download as PNG.

Choosing a chart type

Bar charts compare categories, line charts show trends over a sequence, and pie charts show parts of a whole. Pick the one that matches your message.

Formatting your data

Enter one item per line as label and value. The chart scales automatically to your data range.

How to use Chart & Graph Maker

  1. Enter data lines of label and value.
  2. Pick a chart type.
  3. Download the PNG.
