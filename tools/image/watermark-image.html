Watermark Image: Protect Your Photos with Text or Logo Overlays

Whether you are a photographer protecting portfolio shots, a business branding product images, or a content creator marking drafts, watermarks are a quick and effective way to claim ownership. The Troolify Watermark Image tool lets you add text or logo watermarks to any picture entirely in your browser - no uploads, no accounts, no desktop software required.

What the Watermark Tool Does

Upload an image, then choose between a text watermark (typed words with full font, size, color, opacity and rotation control) or a logo watermark (a second image overlaid on top). Position the watermark on a 9-point grid that covers corners, edges and the center. For maximum coverage, enable diagonal tiling and the tool repeats your watermark in a diagonal pattern across the entire image.

Because all rendering happens via the HTML Canvas API inside your browser tab, your original photos and logos are never sent anywhere.

How to Use the Watermark Tool

  1. Upload your image - click the drop zone and pick a PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF.
  2. Choose watermark type - switch between the Text and Logo tabs.
  3. Customize - set font, size, color, opacity, rotation, shadow and bold/italic for text, or upload a logo and set its width and opacity.
  4. Pick a position - click one of the 9 grid cells to place the watermark.
  5. Optional: enable tiling - toggle Diagonal Tiling for full-image coverage.
  6. Download - preview the result, choose PNG or JPEG, and save the watermarked image.

Tips for Effective Watermarks

  • Use semi-transparency - an opacity of 30-50% protects the image without hiding the subject.
  • Choose a contrasting color - white text on dark images, dark text on bright images, or add a shadow for visibility on any background.
  • Use diagonal tiling for maximum protection - a single corner watermark can be cropped out; tiling covers the whole image.
  • Use PNG logos with transparency - a transparent PNG avoids an ugly white box around your logo.
  • Keep the original - always watermark a copy so you retain a clean, unmarked version of the original.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a watermark to an image without Photoshop?

Use this free online tool: upload your image, type your watermark text or upload a logo, pick a position on the 9-point grid, adjust opacity and rotation, then download the watermarked image. No software installation or account needed.

What is the difference between text and logo watermarks?

Text watermarks use typed words with customizable font, size, color and rotation. Logo watermarks use a second image (such as a brand mark) that is overlaid on the original. Both can be positioned, made semi-transparent, and tiled diagonally.

Is my image uploaded to a server when I add a watermark?

No. The Troolify Watermark Image tool processes everything inside your browser using the HTML Canvas API. Your original image and any logo never leave your device, keeping them completely private.

Can I tile a watermark diagonally across the entire image?

Yes. Enable the Diagonal Tiling toggle and the tool will repeat your text or logo watermark in a diagonal pattern across the whole image. You can still control opacity, rotation and color of each repeated tile.
