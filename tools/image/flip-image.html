Flip Image

The Flip Image tool mirrors any image horizontally (like a bathroom-mirror effect) or flips it vertically (upside-down), producing a new PNG you can download - all processed locally on a canvas.

How to Use

  1. Choose an image from your device.
  2. Pick a direction - horizontal mirror or vertical flip.
  3. Click Flip image and download the result.

Why It Matters

Mirrored photos fix handedness, correct direction-of-light mistakes in composites, and give design assets a second useful variant without a heavyweight editor. Doing it in-browser means your image never leaves the machine.

Contractor Tip

Flip before exporting sprite and icon variants - a horizontally mirrored arrow glyph makes the 'previous' icon you would otherwise draw from scratch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does flipping lose quality?

No more than the PNG export itself - the flip is a lossless mirror of the sampled pixels at the original resolution.
