Photo to Sketch Converter: Pencil Effect

A pencil sketch highlights the structure of a photo. This photo to sketch converter detects edges and renders them as dark lines on white.

Edge detection

Each pixel is compared with its neighbours; where brightness changes sharply, an edge exists. Inverting that edge strength produces dark lines on a light background.

Best results

Photos with clear subjects and moderate contrast sketch best. Very flat or very noisy images produce sparse or scratchy lines.

How to use Photo to Sketch Converter

  1. Choose a photo.
  2. Press Create sketch.
  3. Download the PNG.
