Free Games & Giveaways Finder

The Free Games & Giveaways Finder pulls every active giveaway tracked by GamerPower - free games, beta keys and in-game loot - into one live, filterable list. No API key and no sign-up: choose a platform, choose a type, and click Find giveaways.

How to Use

  1. Choose a platform - PC, Steam, Epic Games Store, GOG, PlayStation, Xbox, Switch, Android, iOS, DRM-Free or itch.io, or leave it on All platforms.
  2. Choose a type and a sort order - full games, in-game loot or beta keys; newest first, highest value first or most popular first.
  3. Click Find giveaways - the live list appears with thumbnail, worth, platform tags, claim counts and end dates.
  4. Open How to claim for the exact steps, then hit Claim giveaway to jump to the offer page.

Why It Matters

Good giveaways vanish within hours and are scattered across a dozen stores. One filtered, refreshed feed means you spot the active offers in time - and the worth estimation at the top shows what the current pool is actually worth.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this really free to use?

Yes - the GamerPower API is free and needs no API key, and Troolify charges nothing. The giveaway hosts themselves may still require their own free account to hand over a key.

Where do the giveaways come from?

Every listing comes from GamerPower, a giveaway tracker covering free games, beta keys and in-game loot across stores and platforms. Attribution and a link to GamerPower are shown with every result.

Why do offers disappear quickly?

Giveaways end by key count or by date, and the pool changes constantly. Sort by newest and refresh often - every search fetches live data straight from the API.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes - the tool is responsive and runs in any modern browser, with nothing to install.

Conclusion

Every active free game, beta key and loot drop in one filtered live list - powered by the free GamerPower API.
