Emoji Picker - Search, Browse & Copy Emojis

Browse, search and copy emojis - with categories, a recent list and click-to-copy.

One-click emoji copying

Stop hunting through the OS emoji panel. This picker groups hundreds of popular emojis into categories, lets you search by keyword (try "cat" or "fire"), remembers your recents locally, and copies any emoji to the clipboard with a single click.

How to use Emoji Picker

  1. Search by name or browse a category
  2. Click an emoji to copy it
  3. Check the Recents tab for your last picks

Tips for getting accurate results

  • Recents are stored in your browser only (localStorage)
  • Search matches both the emoji name and its category
  • Emojis render in color on all modern systems

Frequently asked questions

Where are my recents stored?

Locally in your browser - never uploaded.

Can I paste them anywhere?

Yes - anywhere that accepts Unicode text: chat, docs, code comments.
