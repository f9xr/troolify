Banana for Scale Converter

The Banana for Scale Converter answers the internet's favourite measuring question: how many bananas is that? It loads the full unit catalogue from the free Tasta Space hub - real SI-derived units alongside smoots, double-decker buses, football pitches, Olympic pools, warhols and floppy disks - and converts between any pair of them.

How to Use

  1. Type a value - any number, decimals welcome.
  2. Pick the from and to units - the selects group units by dimension (length, area, volume, time, mass, data); changing one side to a new dimension automatically re-targets the other.
  3. Click Convert - the result appears as a big number, a ready-made sentence, and the documented basis of each silly unit with its source.
  4. Swap units to flip the direction instantly.

Why It Matters

Silly units only work when their basis is honest. Every comedic unit here carries its real definition - the 18 cm USDA medium banana, Oliver Smoot's 1.7018 m height, the 8.4 m Routemaster bus - so the joke still measures something.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do the units come from?

The unit catalogue is fetched live from the Tasta Space hub: 70+ units across length, area, volume, time, mass and data, each with its SI factor and, for the silly ones, a documented basis and source link.

How accurate are the silly units?

As accurate as their definitions allow - real units use exact SI conversion factors, and each silly unit states its reference (for example a medium banana at 18 cm) right next to the result.

Can I use this for serious work?

You can, but your accountant may object - the conversions are mathematically correct, yet bananas remain a questionable unit of accountancy.

Is it free and private?

Yes - no API key, no sign-up, no storage on Troolify. The value and units you pick are sent to the free public API to compute the answer.

Conclusion

Bananas, smoots and Routemasters as honest units of measure - powered by the free Tasta Space API.
