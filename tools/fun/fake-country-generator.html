Fake Country Generator

The Fake Country Generator invents a nation that never existed, complete with a procedurally drawn flag, a map silhouette, a capital, a currency with exchange rates, a motto in Latin with its translation, and a national dish worth pretending to visit. Everything comes from the free Tasta Space hub, which filters real country names out so the results stay fictional.

How to Use

  1. Optionally type a seed - any short text; the same seed always rebuilds the same country, so a story bible stays consistent. Leave it blank for a surprise.
  2. Click Generate a country - the flag, stats, currency and culture appear instantly.
  3. Copy the permalink to share or revisit this exact country, or open the PNG flag and map directly.

Why It Matters

Writers, game masters and worldbuilders need plausible filler states fast. A seed-stable country with a flag and a motto is a ready-made footnote for any fictional map - no naming muscle required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Could I get a real country by accident?

The hub filters known real country names before generating, so results stay fictional. If a real one ever slips through, the API authors remove it.

What does the seed do?

The seed deterministically drives every choice - name, flag colours, layout, statistics. The same seed always returns the same country, which is ideal for keeping a fictional world consistent.

Can I use these countries in my stories or games?

Yes - they are generated fiction with no real-world counterpart. Publish, worldbuild and game with them freely.

Is it free?

Completely - no key, no sign-up. Each generation is one request to the free public Tasta Space API.

Conclusion

A whole fictional nation - flag, motto and walnut-sauce duck included - in one click.
