Life Stats Calculator

The Life Stats Calculator turns a birthday into a set of wonderfully useless numbers, computed by the free Tasta Space hub: your age in seconds and in full moons survived, kilometres travelled around the Sun and around the galaxy, estimated heartbeats and breaths, Fridays the 13th outlasted - and your age on every planet, from Mercury to Pluto. It finishes with your next planetary milestone and a countdown to it.

How to Use

  1. Enter your date of birth - day, month and year.
  2. Click Show my life - the full dashboard appears instantly.
  3. Open a milestone certificate - each upcoming planetary birthday links to a printable certificate.

Why It Matters

Numbers this large stop meaning anything at human scale - which is exactly the point. A billion seconds lands around age 31; a single orbit of the galaxy is a rounding error on your address. It is perspective, with a countdown.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are the numbers?

Days and seconds are exact for a midnight-to-midnight birth date. Heartbeats, breaths and the kilometre counts are steady-rate estimates from documented adult ranges (75 bpm, 15 breaths per minute), clearly flagged as such.

How is age on planets calculated?

Days alive are divided by each planet's sidereal year - 88 days on Mercury, 24.8 years on Pluto - so a 40-year-old Earthling is roughly 165 Mercury years old.

What is a gigasecond?

One billion seconds - about 31.7 years. The tool shows the exact date you passed (or will pass) your first gigasecond.

Is my birth date private?

It is sent to the free Tasta Space API to compute the stats and stored nowhere - not by Troolify and not by the hub beyond the request itself.

Conclusion

Your lifetime, rescaled into full moons and planetary birthdays - one date in, one dashboard out.
