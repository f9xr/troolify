Random Hobby Generator: Something New to Try

Stuck for something to do that is not a screen? This random hobby generator serves up pastimes across five types - creative, active, social, relaxing and learning - each with a one-line idea to get you started.

Five types of pastime

Pick a type to match your mood, or leave it on "Any" for the widest spread. Creative hobbies make things, active hobbies move you, social hobbies bring people together, relaxing hobbies slow the day down, and learning hobbies stretch a skill. The one-line idea with each result - row by patient row, chase the light - is there to lower the barrier to starting.

Using it well

Try the first suggestion that raises an eyebrow rather than the one that sounds easiest. If a hobby appeals but feels too big, search for a beginner version - most have a cheap starter kit or a five-minute version. Generative prompts like this work best when you actually attempt one, not when you keep rerolling.

How to use Random Hobby Generator

  1. Choose a type, or leave it on "Any".
  2. Set how many hobbies you want.
  3. Press Generate.
  4. Pick one and give it a try this week.

Frequently asked questions

Yes. Pick creative, active, social, relaxing or learning, or leave it on "Any".

Yes. Most have cheap starter versions; the note gives you a first step.

Will hobbies repeat?

Not within a single run.

Is it free?

Yes. No sign-up and no limits.

Do you store anything?

No. Everything is generated in your browser.
