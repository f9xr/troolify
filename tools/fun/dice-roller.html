Dice Roller: Roll Any Die, Any Number of Times

Forgot your dice bag, or need to roll ten d20s in a row without the physics? This online dice roller handles up to 20 dice with anywhere from 2 to 1000 sides, shows every individual result, and keeps a tidy history of recent rolls. It is built for tabletop gaming, board games and just-for-fun dice math - all in your browser.

How to Use the Dice Roller

  1. Set the number of dice (from 1 to 20).
  2. Set the sides per die (from 2 to 1000) - perfect for d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20 or d100 systems.
  3. Hit Roll to see each die, the total, the highest and the lowest values.
  4. Use Roll ×10 for a burst of results and watch the last ten rolls in the history panel.

Rolling a single d20 for an attack, or five dice for a Yahtzee-style game - either way the summary tells you what you need instantly.

Randomness You Can Trust

This tool uses the Web Crypto API's crypto.getRandomValues, a cryptographic-grade random source backed by the operating system, instead of the weaker Math.random used by many dice apps. Each side is equally likely within statistical tolerance, which is exactly what you want when a critical hit is on the line.

For other decision-making fun, try the Coin Flip Simulator for a two-sided version, and when letters are the puzzle, the Anagram Solver has your back.

Dice Notation Refresher

Tabletop players write dice as XdY - "2d6" means two six-sided dice. This roller accepts any combination within its limits, so "3d8", "1d20" and even a weird "5d12" are all a single click away. The highest and lowest values help you apply rules like advantage (take the highest of two rolls) or dropping the lowest die.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this dice roller truly random?

As close as a browser can get. The tool uses the Web Crypto API (crypto.getRandomValues), a cryptographic-grade random source, rather than the less predictable Math.random.

Can I roll dice with more or fewer than 6 sides?

Yes. The sides control accepts any value from 2 to 1000, so you can roll d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20, d100 and beyond for any tabletop system.

How many dice can I roll at once?

Up to 20 dice in a single roll. Each die is shown individually, and the total, highest and lowest values are reported so you can apply game rules such as advantage, disadvantage or drop-lowest.

Is the Troolify dice roller safe to use?

Yes. Every roll happens locally inside your browser tab. No results are uploaded, stored, or transmitted to a server, so your rolls stay completely private.

Conclusion

Whether you are settling a dispute with a d20 or testing dice probabilities for a homebrew game, a fair, fast and private dice roller is a must. Roll up, track your totals, and let the dice decide - right from this page.
