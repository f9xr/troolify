Random Country Generator: A Nation and Its Capital

Pick a country at random for a quiz, a travel daydream or a geography game. This random country generator draws from every continent and can show the capital city and region alongside the name.

A fair, worldwide draw

The pool spans North and South America, Europe, Africa, Asia and Oceania, so a run feels global rather than Euro-centric. Choose how much detail to show: just the country, the country with its continent, or the full line with its capital. Duplicates are avoided within a single run, which makes it easy to build a round of ten distinct questions.

Quizzes, lessons and travel

Quiz masters use it to draw "name the capital" questions. Teachers use it to assign countries for a geography project. Travellers use it for a bit of armchair tourism. Writers use it to place a scene somewhere unfamiliar and specific.

How to use Random Country Generator

  1. Choose the level of detail.
  2. Set how many countries you want.
  3. Press Generate.
  4. Copy the list for your quiz or project.

Frequently asked questions

Are all continents represented?

Yes. The pool includes countries from the Americas, Europe, Africa, Asia and Oceania.

Can it show the capital city?

Yes. Pick the full detail level and each result shows the capital.

Will countries repeat?

Not within a single run.

Is it suitable for a quiz?

Yes - it draws the questions; you keep the score.

Do you store anything?

No. Everything is generated in your browser.
