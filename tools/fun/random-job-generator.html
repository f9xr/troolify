Random Job Generator: A Random Occupation in One Click

Draw a job at random for a game of charades, a story, a careers lesson or a bit of daydreaming. This random job generator pulls from seven broad fields - tech, creative, trades, business, science and public service - and labels each pick with its category.

Seven fields, one draw

Jobs are grouped so you can keep things coherent or mix them freely. The tech pool is modern and digital, the trades pool is hands-on, and the creative pool spans design, writing, music and games. Each result shows its field, which is useful when you want a themed round rather than a total surprise.

Where it comes in handy

Teachers use it for "when I grow up" activities and interview role-play. Writers use it to give a background character a believable day job. Party games use it for charades and quick-fire guessing. And sometimes you just want a prompt to think about a career you had not considered.

How to use Random Job Generator

  1. Pick a field, or leave it on "Any".
  2. Set how many jobs you want.
  3. Press Generate.
  4. Copy the list for your game or project.

Frequently asked questions

How many jobs are included?

Dozens spread across seven fields, from tech and trades to science and public service.

Can I pick just one field?

Yes. Choose a field, or leave it on "Any" to draw from all of them.

Will the same job repeat?

Not within a single run.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes. All entries are ordinary, real-world jobs.

Do you store anything?

No. Everything is generated in your browser.
