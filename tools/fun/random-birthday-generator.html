Random Birthday Generator: A Realistic Random Date of Birth

Need a random birthday for a character sheet, a lucky draw or a game? This random birthday generator produces believable dates of birth - correct weekdays, correct leap years - and can attach the zodiac sign too.

Believable dates, not just any numbers

The generator draws a year between 1950 and 2011, then a real day within that month. February gets 29 days in leap years and 28 in the rest, so you never get an impossible date like 31 April or 30 February. The weekday is computed from the actual date, which means the day shown always matches the calendar.

Day of the week and zodiac

Each result can include the day of the week the person was born on and their zodiac sign, calculated from the precise date boundaries. That is the sort of detail that makes a fictional character feel real - and it is handy for classroom astrology or trivia games.

How to use Random Birthday Generator

  1. Decide whether to show the zodiac sign.
  2. Set how many birthdays you need.
  3. Press Generate.
  4. Copy the list to use in your project.

Frequently asked questions

Can the generator produce impossible dates?

No. Month lengths and leap years are handled, so every date is real.

What ages does it cover?

Anyone born between 1950 and 2011, roughly pre-teens to mid-seventies today.

How is the zodiac calculated?

From the standard date boundaries for each sign, using the generated month and day.

Is it the same as a random age generator?

It generates a full date, from which the age follows; you can read the year to work out age.

Do you save the dates?

No. Everything is generated in your browser.

Every date is plausible and every weekday is correct. The random birthday generator is the quickest way to get a believable date of birth. For a themed name instead, try the Greek name generator.
