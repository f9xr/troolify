Random Drawing Generator: Beat the Blank Page

The hardest part of drawing is deciding what to draw. This random drawing generator gives you a subject and a twist - "a paper lantern, from an unusual angle" - so you can start making marks instead of deliberating.

A subject plus a constraint

Each prompt pairs a subject from one of four categories - objects, creatures, people or scenes - with a modifier that changes how you approach it. The modifier is the important part: a five-minute limit, a three-colour palette or an odd angle forces decisions and teaches you faster than a free choice would.

Turning prompts into practice

Use one prompt a day as a warm-up, or draw the same prompt three times with different modifiers to compare approaches. For challenge communities, generate a list and share it as that week's set. The randomness keeps your practice broad, hitting subjects you would otherwise avoid.

How to use Random Drawing Generator

  1. Choose a subject type or leave it on "Any".
  2. Set how many prompts you want.
  3. Press Generate.
  4. Sketch the first prompt without second-guessing it.

Tips for getting accurate results

  • Draw the first prompt immediately, before you talk yourself out of it.
  • Repeat a prompt with different modifiers to compare results.
  • Use the modifier as a rule, not a suggestion.

Key takeaways

Frequently asked questions

Is this suitable for beginners?

Yes. Prompts are simple, everyday subjects that anyone can attempt.

Can I choose what kind of thing to draw?

Yes. Pick objects, creatures, people or scenes, or leave it fully random.

What if I do not like the prompt?

Generate again, or accept the challenge - the point is to draw something you would not normally choose.

Can I use these prompts for a class or challenge?

Yes, they are free to use and share.

Do you store anything?

No. Prompts are generated in your browser.

Conclusion

Stop planning and start drawing. The random drawing generator hands you a subject and a reason to begin. For a different kind of prompt, try the random theme generator.
