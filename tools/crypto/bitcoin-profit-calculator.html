Bitcoin Profit Calculator: Know What Your Trade Really Made

Bitcoin moves fast, and so do the mental calculations. Selling at a higher price than you paid feels like a win, but once fees and the exact amount held are factored in, the true number can look quite different. This Bitcoin profit calculator turns buy price, sell price and amount into a clear profit or loss figure - instantly and entirely in your browser.

How to Use the Bitcoin Profit Calculator

  1. Enter the price you paid per Bitcoin, in USD.
  2. Enter the price you sold at, or the current price for an unrealised view.
  3. Enter the amount you hold in BTC - fractions like 0.5 work fine.
  4. Optionally add a trading fee percentage to see the net figure after exchange fees.

The calculator returns total invested, total received, gross profit or loss, return percentage and the net result after fees.

The Profit Formula

The core calculation is simple: profit = (sell price − buy price) × amount in BTC. The total invested is buy price × amount, and total received is sell price × amount. When a fee percentage is given, the tool applies it to both sides of the trade and subtracts it, so the net profit reflects the cost of actually executing the round trip.

Why Fees Matter More Than You Think

At first glance a 0.1% fee sounds negligible. But it is charged on the notional size of each trade, so a $20,000 position costs $20 to open and another $20 to close before you have made anything. Frequent traders paying the fee on both sides of every trade give up a meaningful slice of their gains - this calculator makes that cost visible.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is Bitcoin profit calculated?

Profit equals the sale value minus the buy cost: (sell price - buy price) × amount in BTC. Trading fees are then subtracted to give the net figure.

Do trading fees affect my Bitcoin profit?

Yes. Most exchanges charge a fee percentage on each trade. Over many trades those small percentages add up and can meaningfully eat into (or inflate the loss of) a position.

What is a good Bitcoin profit percentage?

That depends on your entry point and time horizon. Long-term holders measure multi-year returns, while short-term traders often measure daily or weekly moves. Compare your result against a simple buy-and-hold benchmark.

Is the Troolify Bitcoin profit calculator safe to use?

Yes. Every calculation runs locally inside your browser tab. Your prices and amounts are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted to a server, so your trading figures stay completely private.
