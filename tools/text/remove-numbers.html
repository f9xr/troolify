Remove Numbers from Text: Clean Digits Out of Any Copy

Numbers creep into text in ways we barely notice - phone numbers pasted into notes, order IDs glued to product names, serials and quantities scattered through a document. When you need the words without the digits, retyping is not the answer; this tool is.

About the Number Remover

Remove all digits deletes every digit character, leaving letters and punctuation intact - the fastest way to an all-word version of a paste. Keep digits, remove letters does the opposite, preserving numbers so you can extract quantities, IDs or dates wrapped in words. Remove numbers standing alone only is the surgical option: it removes blocks of digits that sit on their own (like 123 in "room 123") while leaving part-of-word numbers (Barcelona08, 7th, v2) untouched.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Paste your text into the input box.
  2. Pick a mode - remove everything numeric, keep the digits, or target standalone numbers.
  3. Check the live count to see how many digits (or letters) were removed.
  4. Copy the cleaned text and carry on.

Why It Matters

Marketers anonymize before publishing, editors normalize user-submitted copy, and data handlers wipe personal identifiers before analysis. Doing it in the browser keeps the operation private - a real advantage when the text contains customer data. For adjacent cleanup, the Replace Text tool can swap remaining patterns, and the Space Remover finishes the tidy-up.

Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as a number standing alone?

A standalone number is a block of digits surrounded by non-letter boundaries, such as 123 in "room 123". Digits attached to letters (Barcelona08, 7th, v2) are kept, because they are part of a word or identifier rather than a distinct number.

Can I keep only the digits and remove all letters?

Yes - switch the mode to "Keep digits, remove letters". This strips the A-Z and a-z alphabet, leaving numbers and punctuation, which is handy for extracting phone numbers or IDs cluttered with words.

Is the Troolify version safe for my data?

Yes. Everything runs locally inside your browser tab - nothing you paste or upload is sent to a server, so your text never leaves your device.
