Palindrome Checker: Words That Read the Same Both Ways

Radar. Level. A man, a plan, a canal - Panama. Palindromes are a long-standing favourite of writers, word-game players and puzzle lovers. This checker tells you in an instant whether a word or phrase qualifies, and shows exactly why.

What Is a Palindrome?

A palindrome is a word, phrase, number or other sequence that reads identically forwards and backwards once you ignore case, spaces and punctuation. Single words like racecar are palindromes, and so are full sentences like Never odd or even.

Numeric palindromes exist, too - 12321 reads the same reversed - and even dates such as 12-02-2021 can be palindromic depending on their format.

How the Checker Decides

  1. Normalize - text is lowercased and accents are removed so résumeé-style spelling differences do not matter.
  2. Strip - spaces, punctuation and symbols are removed, keeping only letters and digits.
  3. Compare - the cleaned string is reversed and compared with itself.

The tool also displays both the normalized string and its reverse, so you can see the symmetry at a glance.

How to Use the Palindrome Checker

Just start typing or paste any text. The result updates live - a clear green or red verdict, plus live counts of letters checked, total characters and words. Empty or symbol-only input shows a neutral waiting state rather than a false positive.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a palindrome?

A palindrome is a word, phrase, number or sequence that reads the same forwards and backwards after ignoring case, spaces and punctuation. Examples include radar, level and A man, a plan, a canal - Panama.

How does the checker decide the result?

The text is lowercased, accents are removed, and every character that is not a letter or digit is stripped. The remaining string is then compared with its reversed form. If they match and are not empty, it is a palindrome.

Are numbers considered palindromes too?

Yes. Numeric palindromes such as 12321 and 1001 are accepted, since digits are kept during normalization. Dates like 12-02-2021 also work when formatting such as hyphens is ignored.

Is the Troolify palindrome checker private?

Yes. The check runs entirely inside your browser tab. Your text is never uploaded, stored, or transmitted to a server.
