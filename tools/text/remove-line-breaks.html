Remove Line Breaks: Clean Up Text Instantly in Your Browser

When you copy text from a PDF, email, or web page, line breaks often appear in unexpected places. This free online tool lets you remove those unwanted breaks and join lines back into clean, flowing paragraphs - all without uploading a single byte to a server.

About the Remove Line Breaks Tool

The tool strips carriage-return (CR) characters and splits your text on every line-feed boundary, giving you an accurate count of how many lines exist in the source. It then joins them back together using the mode you choose: a normal space, a single compressed space, no separator at all, or a comma followed by a space.

Because everything runs in the browser, your text never leaves your device. There are no uploads, no accounts, and no waiting.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Paste your text into the input textarea - any size works for typical editing tasks.
  2. Choose a join mode - Space inserts a space between each line, Single space collapses to one space regardless of original gap, Nothing joins lines end-to-end, and Comma + Space adds a comma separator.
  3. Read the stats - the line-in and line-out counters update instantly so you know exactly how many breaks were removed.
  4. Copy the result with one click and paste it into your document, editor or terminal.

Why Removing Line Breaks Matters

Clean text is easier to read, search, and process. Whether you are preparing data for import, normalizing pasted content for a blog post, or cleaning up CSV exports, removing stray line breaks saves time and prevents formatting errors.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of line breaks can this tool remove?

The tool removes both CR (carriage return) and LF (line feed) characters, splitting on every newline boundary. You can then join lines with a space, remove gaps entirely, or separate them with a comma.

Is my text uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens locally inside your browser. Nothing you paste is ever sent to a server, so your text remains completely private.

Can I see how many lines were removed?

Yes. The tool displays a stat row showing the number of lines in the input and the number of lines in the output, so you can verify exactly how many breaks were stripped.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Like all Troolify tools this runs on desktop and mobile browsers with no install and no account required.

Conclusion

Removing line breaks is a small task that can save you minutes of manual cleanup. With instant live output, clear stats and one-click copy, this tool makes the job effortless - and your text stays on your device the entire time.
