Paragraphs to Single Line: Flatten Text for Easy Reuse

Multi-line text is great for reading but awkward for search bars, text boxes and single-field inputs. This tool folds each paragraph into one continuous line, collapsing its internal line breaks into clean spaces.

About the Paragraphs to Single Line Tool

The tool recognises paragraphs as groups of lines separated by blank lines. Each paragraph's line breaks are collapsed into single spaces, so a multiple-line paragraph becomes one line. Then, depending on the mode, paragraphs are joined by a single space or a double space.

All this happens locally in your browser, so your text stays private and results appear instantly as you type.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Paste your text with its paragraph breaks into the input box.
  2. Choose a join mode - a single space keeps one continuous line; double space keeps paragraph boundaries visible.
  3. Preview the output on the single line below.
  4. Copy the result and paste it into the target field.

Why Flattening Paragraphs Helps

Single-line text is easy to paste into search boxes, ticket systems, chat fields and single-row tables, and it previews cleanly in reports and summaries. By normalising whitespace, this tool also makes the text consistent and predictable for further processing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are paragraphs detected in the input?

Paragraphs are separated by one or more blank lines. Within each paragraph, line breaks are collapsed into single spaces so each paragraph becomes one line.

What is the difference between the two join options?

The 'space' option joins paragraphs with a single space (one line of continuous text). The 'double space' option inserts two spaces between paragraphs, making the paragraph boundaries slightly more visible while still keeping everything on one line.

Will my paragraph text be altered?

Only the line breaks and repeated spaces inside each paragraph are normalised into clean single spaces. The actual words and sentences are preserved as written.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Like all Troolify tools this runs on desktop and mobile browsers with no install and no account required.

Conclusion

Flattening multi-line paragraphs into single lines makes text far easier to paste, search and reuse. With instant collapse, flexible paragraph joining and complete privacy, this tool turns any block of prose into a clean line in seconds.
