Remove Spaces Online: Clean Whitespace in One Click

Extra spaces hide in the most inconvenient places - copied CSV cells padded for alignment, form fields that mangle pasted text, and block quotes pasted from email with ragged indentation. None of it is worth fixing by hand.

About the Space Cleaner

Three modes cover almost every whitespace problem. Remove all whitespace deletes spaces, tabs and newlines outright, joining a paste into one continuous string - useful for hashes, codes and single-line exports. Collapse to single spaces squeezes runs of spaces and tabs down to one space while keeping line endings intact, so paragraphs survive. Trim edges only removes leading and trailing whitespace on every line and around the whole block, leaving the inner content untouched.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Paste the offending text into the input box.
  2. Choose a mode - remove everything, collapse runs, or just trim edges.
  3. Scan the instant output to confirm the whitespace is gone.
  4. Copy the result and paste it where it belongs.

Why Whitespace Cleanup Matters

Invisible characters cause visible breakage. A single concordant space can break a CSV import, a shell command, or a comparison in a script. Cleaning text locally means pastes stay private - try it before the Text to Slug Converter if URL fragments end up with gaps, or pair it with the Line Breaks Remover to flatten whole blocks into a single paragraph.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does removing all whitespace delete my paragraph breaks?

Yes - the "Remove all whitespace" mode also strips tabs and newlines, so every line joins into one continuous string. If you want to keep paragraph breaks, use "Collapse to single spaces", which merges runs of spaces while preserving line endings.

Is the cleaned result safe to copy for codes or sensitive strings?

The tool only removes whitespace - it never reorders or otherwise alters your characters, and everything runs locally, so even sensitive strings are processed without leaving your browser.

Is the Troolify version safe for my data?

Yes. Everything runs locally inside your browser tab - nothing you paste or upload is sent to a server, so your text never leaves your device.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Like all Troolify tools this runs on desktop and mobile browsers with no install and no account required.

Conclusion

Whitespace should be invisible - not a chore. One paste, one mode, one copy, and your text is clean enough for any platform, all processed locally in your browser with zero data retention.
