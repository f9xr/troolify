Word Frequency Counter: Reveal What Your Text Is Really About

Every piece of writing leans on a handful of words far more than the rest. Finding those words quickly tells you what a text emphasizes - and whether it emphasizes the right things. This word frequency counter ranks every word by count, with clear percentages, in real time.

What the Word Frequency Counter Shows

As you type or paste, the tool splits your text into words and builds a live table ordered from most to least frequent. Each row shows the word, how many times it appeared, and its percentage share of the text, with a bar that makes the spread instantly visible.

A summary row keeps you oriented: total words counted, the number of unique words, the single most-used word, and what share of your text the top ten words account for.

Refining the Results with Filters

  • Minimum word length - set 3 or 4 to drop tiny filler words such as it, to, and at.
  • Ignore common words - strip a curated list of stop words (the, and, of, to, in…) so meaningful vocabulary rises to the top.

Both filters re-run the whole analysis instantly, so you can dial in a clean keyword picture in seconds.

How to Use the Frequency Counter

  1. Paste your text into the input box - essay, article, transcript, or dataset.
  2. Set a minimum word length and toggle the common-word filter as needed.
  3. Scan the ranked table for surprises, overused words, or missing keywords.

Everything updates live, so there is no button to press and no page to reload.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a word frequency counter?

A word frequency counter counts how many times each word appears in a piece of text and ranks the results from most to least frequent. It is used for keyword analysis, writing review, plagiarism checks, and studying vocabulary use.

What are stop words?

Stop words are very common words such as the, and, of, to and in. They appear in nearly every sentence but carry little meaning, so analysts often filter them out to reveal which important words appear most often.

Is the Troolify word frequency counter private?

Yes. The analysis runs entirely inside your browser tab. Your text is never uploaded, stored, or transmitted to a server, so essays, drafts, and confidential documents stay on your device.

How are the percentages calculated?

Each word's percentage is its count divided by the total number of counted words, multiplied by 100. Raising the minimum length or enabling the stop-word filter changes the total, so percentages shift to reflect the words that remain.
