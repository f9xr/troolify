Sentences Counter: See Your Sentence Structure at a Glance

A paragraph built entirely from long, winding sentences reads like a wall of text - even when every sentence is grammatically perfect. Sentence statistics turn that subjective impression into numbers you can act on.

About the Statistics

The counter splits your text on sentence-ending punctuation and reports four numbers live: sentences, words, the average words per sentence, and the character length of the longest sentence. Because it masks common abbreviations like Mr., Dr., e.g. and i.e., a name such as "Dr. Smith" counts as one sentence instead of being sliced in the middle.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Type or paste your text - there is nothing to press, the stats update as you go.
  2. Read the four numbers below the box: sentences, words, average, longest.
  3. Spot run-on sentences by watching the longest-sentence figure climb.
  4. Copy the summary into a report, a readiness checklist or a client email.

Why Sentence Length Matters

Readability research consistently associates longer sentences with harder reading. When your average swings far past 20 words or a single sentence dwarfs the rest, it is usually worth splitting. The same privacy that protects drafts in the Word Counter applies here: everything is counted locally, so a manuscript or a client brief never leaves your machine. For tightening the longest sentences themselves, the Replace Text tool makes surgical edits fast.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do abbreviations like Dr. or e.g. affect the count?

The counter masks common abbreviations (Mr., Mrs., Dr., e.g., i.e. and more) so their periods are not mistaken for sentence ends. That way a name like Dr. Smith stays inside one sentence instead of splitting into two.

What does the longest-sentence measure tell me?

It reports the character length of your longest sentence, a quick proxy for readability. Very long sentences are harder to scan, so spotting them helps you split run-ons and keep prose comfortable to read.

Is the Troolify version safe for my data?

Yes. Everything runs locally inside your browser tab - nothing you paste or upload is sent to a server, so your text never leaves your device.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Like all Troolify tools this runs on desktop and mobile browsers with no install and no account required.

Conclusion

Sentence structure is easier to fix once you can see it. Four numbers give you everything you need to balance your paragraphs, trim the run-ons, and keep readers with you to the final period - all computed privately in your browser.
