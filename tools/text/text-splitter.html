Text Splitter: Turn Any Delimited Text Into a Clean List

Lists arrive glued together more often than not: a comma-separated row from a spreadsheet, a paragraph of tags, or a block of text where each item sits on its own line. This text splitter cuts the text into parts on the delimiter you choose, so you get one item per line ready to paste into anything.

Choosing the right delimiter

A delimiter is simply the character or pattern that separates one item from the next. Commas separate CSV fields, semicolons appear in lists that already contain commas, and a newline separates the lines of a column copied from a document. The tool covers the common ones directly and adds a custom option for anything else, from a pipe to a multi-character marker such as ::.

Whitespace splitting is the flexible option: it treats any run of spaces, tabs or line breaks as one separator, which is ideal for text copied out of a PDF where spacing is inconsistent.

Trimming and removing empty parts

Real text is messy. Splitting a, b, c, on the comma leaves stray spaces and a trailing empty item. Turning on Trim each part removes the leading and trailing whitespace from each piece, and Remove empty parts drops the blanks that a trailing delimiter or a double newline would produce. Together they turn ragged input into a tidy list.

How to use Text Splitter

  1. Paste the text you want to split.
  2. Pick a delimiter, or choose Custom delimiter and type your own.
  3. Leave Trim and Remove empty parts on for the cleanest result.
  4. Copy the one-per-line output and paste it into your target.

Tips for getting accurate results

  • If a part contains the delimiter you chose, switch to a rarer one such as the pipe.
  • Keep trim on unless the whitespace itself matters to you.
  • For very large inputs, split on a newline first to avoid creating a single enormous list.

Frequently asked questions

What is a delimiter?

It is the character or pattern that marks where one part ends and the next begins, such as a comma or a newline.

Can I split on more than one character?

Yes. Choose Custom delimiter and type any string, including a multi-character marker.

Does trimming change my content?

It only removes whitespace from the start and end of each part. The words themselves are untouched.

How do I split text where each item is on its own line?

Choose New line and leave Remove empty parts on so blank lines disappear.

Is my text uploaded?

No. Splitting happens in your browser.
