Lorem Ipsum Generator: Filler Text for Every Design

When a mockup needs content and the client copy has not arrived yet, lorem ipsum is the standard stand-in. This generator produces the classic scrambled Latin on demand - by paragraph, sentence or word - so you always have exactly the right amount of placeholder text.

What Is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem ipsum is a jumbled, Latin-like passage that has been the printing industry's standard dummy text since the 1500s. Because it reads like natural language but has no meaning, people judge a layout for its type, spacing and hierarchy rather than being distracted by the words themselves.

It is scrambled from a work by the Roman philosopher Cicero. The deliberate distortion keeps the distribution of letters and word lengths close to real Latin, which is why the result looks convincing at a glance.

Tips for Using Placeholder Text Well

Use paragraphs when testing long-form layouts, sentences when checking line breaks at different widths, and a handful of words for button labels and navigation mockups. Keep filler realistic in length - padding a hero section with one word looks wrong, while a button overflowing with thirty looks just as bad.

For the surrounding workflow, pair filler text with the Word Counter to confirm your dummy copy matches real content sizes, and the Character Case Converter if a mockup calls for all-caps or title-style labels.

Why Keep Dummy Text Meaningless?

The entire purpose is neutrality. Real words invite the eye to read them and spark opinions about content that has not been written yet; a Latin filler has no content to judge. That is why agencies and product teams still reach for lorem ipsum for wireframes, comps and print proofs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is deliberately scrambled Latin-like placeholder text used in design and publishing since the 1500s. Because it has no real meaning, viewers focus on layout, typography and spacing instead of reading the words.

How many words are in a standard lorem ipsum paragraph?

There is no fixed standard, but a typical paragraph runs 25 to 50 words across four to eight sentences. This generator builds each paragraph from random sentence lengths in roughly that range.

Why is lorem ipsum text scrambled?

The scrambling goes back to a 1960s typesetting book that randomized a passage from Cicero to show off type specimens. The result still resembles real Latin closely enough to look natural, which is exactly the point.
