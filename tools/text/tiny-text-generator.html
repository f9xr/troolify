Tiny Text Generator: Shrink Letters to Superscript

Small superscript text adds a distinctive touch to social bios, forum signatures and footnote-style asides. This generator maps every letter and digit to its Unicode small glyph, producing genuinely tiny characters, not just smaller font sizing.

About the Tool

Each lowercase letter, uppercase letter and digit is translated to its superscript Unicode form - a becomes a, and 4 becomes 4. Characters that have no standard tiny glyph, such as punctuation and most symbols, pass through unchanged, so the meaning of your text is never lost even where the mapping ends.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Paste or type your text in the input box.
  2. Watch the tiny output update live as you type.
  3. Copy the result into whatever app or profile you like.

Because the output is real Unicode text, it copies with regular copy-paste and behaves like normal text in almost any field.

Why It Matters

Superscript glyphs give creative headers and signs a distinct look without uploading images or installing fonts. Since the whole conversion happens in your browser, nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere, making it safe even for private drafts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do some characters stay the same size?

Only letters and digits have a standard superscript glyph in Unicode. Punctuation and other symbols (like apostrophes or slashes) have no tiny glyph, so they pass through unchanged and read just like the original text.

Where can I use tiny or superscript text?

Small superscript characters are popular in social media bios, comments, footnotes, chemical formulas and decorative headings. They copy as normal text, so they work almost anywhere text is accepted.

Is this tiny text generator safe for my data?

Yes. Everything runs locally inside your browser tab - nothing you paste is sent to a server, so your text stays private.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Like all Troolify tools this runs on desktop and mobile browsers with no install and no account required.

Conclusion

Making text tiny is one click with this generator. Paste, copy and go - with privacy built in and no accounts needed, it works on every device you use.
