Grade Prediction Calculator: Forecast Your Final

See where a term is heading. This grade prediction calculator blends your graded work with an expected exam score to forecast the final grade.

Weighted forecast

The prediction weights your current average by the completed percentage and your expected exam by what remains. It is a simple, transparent estimate.

The score you need

Solving the same equation for the exam score tells you exactly what to aim for to hit a target grade.

How to use Grade Prediction Calculator

  1. Enter current grade and how much is complete.
  2. Add your expected exam score and target.
  3. Read the prediction and needed score.
