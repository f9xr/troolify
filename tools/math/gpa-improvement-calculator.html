GPA Improvement Calculator: Hit Your Target

Wondering what it takes to lift your average? This GPA improvement calculator shows the remaining average you need to reach a target CGPA.

The target math

Required GPA equals (target x total credits - current x completed credits) / remaining credits. If it exceeds 4.0, the target is out of reach on a 4.0 scale.

Setting realistic targets

The more credits you have already earned, the harder a given CGPA is to move. Early planning has the most leverage.

How to use GPA Improvement Calculator

  1. Enter current CGPA and credits completed.
  2. Set a target and remaining credits.
  3. Read the GPA you must earn.
