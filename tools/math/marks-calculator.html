Marks Calculator: Total, Average and Percentage

Enter your subject marks once and get the total, average and percentage together. This marks calculator handles varying maximums and mixed totals.

Percentage across subjects

When subjects have different maximum marks, percentage is total obtained divided by total maximum, not the plain average of percentages.

Average mark

The simple average divides total marks by the number of subjects. Use it when all subjects share the same maximum.

How to use Marks Calculator

  1. Enter each subject as obtained and max.
  2. Totals update automatically.
  3. Read percentage, total and average.
