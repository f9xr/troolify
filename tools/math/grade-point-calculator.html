Grade Point Calculator: Points and GPA

Grade points are the raw units behind GPA. This grade point calculator multiplies each letter grade value by its credits to total your points.

What grade points are

Each letter grade maps to a value (A = 4.0, B = 3.0). Multiply by credit hours to get grade points; sum them and divide by credits for GPA.

Standard mapping

The 4.0 scale adds plus/minus steps: A- 3.7, B+ 3.3, B- 2.7 and so on. Confirm your institution mapping.

How to use Grade Point Calculator

  1. Enter each course as grade and credits.
  2. Points are summed automatically.
  3. Read total points and GPA.

Frequently asked questions

What is a grade point?

A letter grade value times credit hours.

Does A+ exceed 4.0?

Usually capped at 4.0.

Which scale is standard?

The 4.0 scale is most common in the US.
