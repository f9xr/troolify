Trigonometric Functions Calculator: Every Ratio for Any Angle

Sine, cosine, tangent - and the less common cosecant, secant and cotangent - describe the relationship between an angle and the sides of a right triangle. They power everything from structural engineering and audio processing to astronomy and computer graphics. This calculator computes all six for any angle, in degrees or radians.

The Six Functions Explained

  • Sine - opposite ÷ hypotenuse, with sign matching the y coordinate on the unit circle.
  • Cosine - adjacent ÷ hypotenuse, matching the x coordinate.
  • Tangent - sin ÷ cos, or opposite ÷ adjacent.
  • Cosecant (csc) - the reciprocal of sine, 1/sin.
  • Secant (sec) - the reciprocal of cosine, 1/cos.
  • Cotangent (cot) - the reciprocal of tangent, 1/tan.

How to Use the Calculator

  1. Enter an angle or tap a quick chip (0, 30, 45, 60, 90 degrees).
  2. Pick degrees or radians from the unit dropdown - results update instantly.
  3. Read all six values in the table; undefined results show "-".
  4. Note the quadrant line telling you which ratios are positive at that angle.

When Results Are Undefined

Some angles produce undefined values because they would require division by zero. Tangent and secant are undefined at 90° (and 270°), while cosecant and cotangent are undefined at 0° and 180°. The calculator shows "-" for these rather than a misleading number. Pair it with the Calculator for geometry checks, or convert between measurement systems with the Radians & Degrees Converter when working across units.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the tangent at 90 degrees?

The tangent of 90 degrees is undefined. It would require dividing sine (1) by cosine (0), and division by zero has no real value. The calculator shows '-' instead of a number, just as standard scientific calculators do.

What does quadrant mean here?

The quadrant line shows the signed signs of the trig ratios for the angle's position on the unit circle - Quadrant I (0-90°) all positive, II (90-180°) sine only positive, III (180-270°) tangent only positive, IV (270-360°) cosine only positive.

Are csc, sec and cot just reciprocals of sin, cos and tan?

Yes. Cosecant = 1/sin, secant = 1/cos and cotangent = 1/tan. Where the base function is zero, its reciprocal is undefined, and the calculator shows '-' in that row.

Can I enter radians directly?

Yes. Switch the unit dropdown to radians and type a value such as 1.5708 or use the quick chips, which set 0, 30, 45, 60 and 90 degrees (0, pi/6, pi/4, pi/3 and pi/2 radians).

Conclusion

Whether you are solving triangles, checking a physics problem, or exploring the unit circle, this calculator gives you all six trig ratios at any angle - with quadrant signs and correct undefined handling, entirely in your browser.
