Calculator: Quick Arithmetic Without Leaving Your Browser

Sometimes you just need to add a few numbers, split a bill, or multiply a price by a quantity - and reaching for a physical calculator or a bloated app feels like overkill. This lightweight browser calculator gives you exactly the four operations you need, with a percent key, sign toggle and full keyboard support.

What the Calculator Offers

  • Addition, subtraction, multiplication and division - the four essential operations.
  • Percent key - convert any number to its percentage form in one tap.
  • Sign toggle (+/-) - flip between positive and negative without retyping.
  • Keyboard support - type digits, operators, Enter and Backspace just like a desktop calculator.
  • Division-by-zero guard - dividing by zero shows a clear error instead of crashing.

How to Use It

  1. Enter numbers by clicking the digit buttons or typing on your keyboard.
  2. Pick an operator (+, -, *, /) - the calculator remembers it and shows it in the expression line.
  3. Press = or Enter to see the result immediately.
  4. Press C or Escape to clear and start fresh.

The calculator evaluates operations sequentially, left to right. That means 2 + 3 * 4 gives 20 rather than 14 - the way most people naturally compute in their heads.

Everyday Use Cases

Use it at the grocery store to tally a receipt, split a dinner bill among friends, or convert measurements in the kitchen. Pair it with the Number List Generator for batch arithmetic, or verify larger computed totals with the Area Calculator when working with measurements. For percentage breakdowns, the Percentage Calculator goes deeper with dedicated margin and markup modes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this calculator follow operator precedence?

No. This calculator uses sequential (left-to-right) evaluation: each operation is applied as soon as you press the next operator or equals. This keeps behaviour predictable and matches the mental arithmetic approach most people use for quick everyday calculations.

How do I use the percent key?

The percent key divides the current number by 100, converting it to its decimal form. For example, typing 200 then % displays 2, which you can then multiply or add with another value. It is useful for calculating tips, discounts and tax.

Can I use the keyboard number pad?

Yes. All digit keys, the operator keys (+ - * /), Enter or = for equals, Backspace for delete and Escape for clear work with this calculator. You do not need to click any buttons if you prefer the keyboard.

What happens if I divide by zero?

The display shows 'Error' instead of attempting an infinite or undefined result. You can clear it by pressing C or Escape to start a fresh calculation.

Conclusion

Whether you are splitting a bill, checking a homework answer, or just need a quick number crunch, this calculator keeps things simple, fast and private - nothing ever leaves your browser.
