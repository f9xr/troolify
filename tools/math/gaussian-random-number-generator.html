Gaussian Random Number Generator

This generator produces random values drawn from a normal (Gaussian) distribution using the Box-Muller transform. Where plain random tools spread values evenly, these cluster around your chosen mean and spread according to your standard deviation - the shape behind salaries, test scores and measurement noise.

How to Use

  1. Set the mean - the center of the distribution.
  2. Set the standard deviation - how wide it spreads.
  3. Choose how many values, then click Generate numbers.

Why It Matters

Simulations and forecasts behave differently with normal vs uniform noise. Feeding a model realistic, bell-shaped input makes results believable rather than evenly scattered - and generating it locally keeps the workload on your machine.

Contractor Tip

Around 68% of generated values fall within one standard deviation of the mean - a quick sanity check that the histogram actually looks normal before you trust a simulation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What distribution is used?

A standard normal distribution produced by the Box-Muller transform, scaled to your mean and standard deviation.

Conclusion

Generate normally distributed numbers with any mean and sigma instantly - all in your browser.
