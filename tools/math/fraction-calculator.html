Fraction Calculator: Every Fraction Problem, Solved and Simplified

Fractions are where a lot of people quietly give up on maths, usually because one small step - finding a common denominator, or reducing the answer - gets skipped. This calculator does every step for you and shows the reduced result, so you can see the answer and then check your own working against it.

What Is a Fraction?

A fraction is a number written as a numerator over a denominator: the top tells you how many parts, the bottom tells you how many parts make a whole. 3/4 means three of four equal pieces. The fraction bar is a division sign in disguise, so 3/4 is really 3 ÷ 4, which is why fractions can be rewritten as decimals - 3/4 is 0.75.

How to Use the Fraction Calculator

  1. Choose the operation - add, subtract, multiply or divide.
  2. Type both fractions into the numerator and denominator boxes. Negative numbers are fine.
  3. Read every form of the answer - the reduced fraction, the mixed number, the decimal and the percentage.

The Four Rules in Brief

  • Adding and subtracting: the denominators must match first. Multiply each fraction by the equivalent fraction to reach a common denominator, then add or subtract the numerators only.
  • Multiplying: multiply across the top and multiply across the bottom, then reduce. No common denominator needed.
  • Dividing: keep the first fraction, flip the second one, then multiply. Dividing by 2/3 becomes multiplying by 3/2.

Improper Fractions and Mixed Numbers

5/3 is bigger than one, so it is called improper. The same value written as a mixed number is 1 and 2/3, which reads far more naturally in real situations like cooking or measurements. The same value is also 1.666... as a decimal. All three forms describe one number, and this calculator returns all of them so you can use whichever suits the problem.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you add two fractions?

Find a common denominator, convert both fractions so the bottoms match, then add the numerators and reduce the result. For 1/2 + 1/3 the common denominator is 6, so 1/2 becomes 3/6 and 1/3 becomes 2/6, giving 5/6.

How do you multiply and divide fractions?

Multiply numerators together and denominators together, then reduce. For division, flip the second fraction upside down and multiply, so dividing by 2/3 is the same as multiplying by 3/2.

What does it mean to simplify a fraction?

Simplifying means dividing the numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor until no larger whole number divides both. 6/8 becomes 3/4 because 2 divides both, and 4 is the largest such number. The calculator does this automatically.

Is my work stored or sent anywhere?

No. All fraction arithmetic happens locally in your browser tab. Nothing you type is uploaded, saved or logged, so you can use it for homework without worrying about privacy.

Conclusion

Fractions reward patience far more than talent. Work the simplification step by hand once or twice and the common factors stop being mysterious. Keep this tool beside you for the checking, then close it and do the next set yourself.
