Salary Slip Generator: Produce a Payslip

A salary slip breaks down pay for a period. This salary slip generator lists earnings, deductions and the resulting net pay.

Components of pay

Basic pay plus allowances form gross pay. Deductions for tax, contributions and advances reduce it to net pay.

Keeping records

Payslips support tax filing, loan applications and dispute resolution. Keep a copy for each period.

How to use Salary Slip Generator

  1. Enter employee details.
  2. Add salary components and deductions.
  3. Copy or print the slip.
