Mailto Link Generator: Pre-Filled Email Links in Seconds

Adding a "Contact us" link or a pre-written email on a website used to mean wrestling with URL encoding by hand. This generator builds a correct, fully-encoded mailto: link for you - with recipients, a subject and even a multi-line body - all without leaving your browser.

About the Tool

The generator encodes every part of your message with encodeURIComponent and converts line breaks to %0D%0A, the standard that email clients expect. The result updates live as you type, so you see exactly what you will copy before you copy it.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Fill in To, CC or BCC - combine multiple addresses with commas.
  2. Type a subject and, optionally, a multi-line body.
  3. Watch the link update live in the output box.
  4. Copy the link or click "Test in your mail app" to verify it opens correctly.

Why It Matters

Correct encoding is the difference between a link that works and one that breaks in your visitor's mail client. This tool removes the guesswork, produces links that respect commas, ampersands, spaces and newlines, and keeps your details private since nothing is sent to a server.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a mailto: link?

A mailto: link is a special URL that, when clicked, opens the user's email client with a new message already addressed. It can pre-fill the To, CC, BCC, subject and body using URL-encoded parameters.

Why are line breaks encoded as %0D%0A?

URLs cannot contain raw newlines, so each line break in your body is converted to the percent-encoded sequence %0D%0A (carriage return + line feed). The generator does this automatically so your formatting survives the link.

Do my email details leave my device?

No. The link is built entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded or stored - it only exists in the copied link, which you choose where to paste.

Will the mailto link work on every device?

It works wherever a mail client is configured - desktop apps, webmail or mobile. Note that some apps ignore the body or BCC field, so it is always wise to test the link by clicking 'Test in your mail app'.

Conclusion

A well-formed mailto: link can drive responses and reduce friction for your visitors. With live encoding, one-click copy and a built-in test, this generator makes creating one effortless - and it is entirely private and free.
