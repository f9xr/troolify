Random Choice Generator: Fair Picks From Any List, Instantly

Choosing a restaurant, a quiz question, a team member, or a random sample? Type your list, hit the button, and get a cryptographically fair pick. Every choice is recorded as a chip so you can see the full set of draws this session.

About the Tool

The generator uses crypto.getRandomValues to pick from your items with equal probability for each. Toggle "Allow repeats between picks" to control whether the same item can appear again on a later draw, exactly like drawing with or without replacement from a bag.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Type or paste your items, one per line.
  2. Decide on repeats - leave the box checked to allow repeats between picks, or untick it to cycle through the list without repeats.
  3. Press Pick Random each time you need a new result.
  4. Watch the chips grow with every outcome, and copy any single pick.

Why It Matters

Whether settling a debate or producing neutral sample data, trustworthy randomness matters. Secure, browser-based picks remove guesswork, keep your items private, and give you a transparent record of every draw - all without any uploads or accounts.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is a random item chosen?

Each pick uses crypto.getRandomValues, the browser's cryptographically secure pseudo-random number generator. Every item in your list has an equal, unbiased chance of being selected.

What does 'Allow repeats between picks' do?

By default a repeat is allowed - the same item can be picked again on a later spin, like drawing from a deck with replacement. Turn it off if you prefer every item to appear at most once before the list is exhausted.

Where do I see my past picks?

Every result is kept as an on-screen chip beneath the output for the current session, and the exact time of the last pick is shown. History clears when you close the tab.

Is my list uploaded anywhere?

No. Your items are processed entirely in your browser and never leave your device, so nothing is stored, shared or uploaded.

Conclusion

From dinner plans to random sampling, this generator gives you fair, secure, transparent picks - with a running record of every draw. It is private, instant and completely free.
