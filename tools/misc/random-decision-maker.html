    Random Decision Maker: Fair, Bias-Free Choices With One Click

    What to eat, which movie to watch, who goes first, where to meet - decision paralysis is real. This tool removes the guesswork: you supply the options and cryptographically secure randomness does the rest, giving you a fair result every time.

    About the Tool

    Every pick is drawn using crypto.getRandomValues, the same secure random source used for encryption. Each option gets an equal, unbiased chance. You can ask for multiple picks at once, keep them unique within a decision, and review your decision history for the rest of the session.

    How to Use This Tool

    1. Type your options, one per line, in the input box.
    2. Choose how many options to pick at once (default 1).
    3. Keep "Unique picks" ticked to avoid repeating an option in the same decision.
    4. Press Make Decision - the verdict appears large, and past picks are kept in the session history.

    Why It Matters

    Quickly resolving everyday or group decisions saves time and avoids arguments. When the outcome should be neutral - splitting tasks, picking a winner, or settling dinner - a crypto-fair picker is the most trustworthy coin you can flip, and it never leaves your device.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How is the decision randomness generated?

    Every pick uses crypto.getRandomValues, the browser's cryptographically secure pseudo-random number generator. Unlike Math.random(), its results are seeded from operating-system entropy, so each option has a genuinely fair chance.

    Can I pick more than one option?

    Yes. Set 'Options to pick' to 2 or more. With 'Unique picks' enabled the tool draws without replacing any option, like dealing distinct results; disable it to allow the same option to appear more than once.

    Is this suitable for choosing winners or prizes?

    Yes. The cryptographic-grade randomness and optional unique-picks mode make it well suited to fair draws, raffle winners and giveaways - all computed privately on your device.

    Is my data sent anywhere?

    No. Your options and results stay entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored or shared - even the decision history is session-only and cleared when you close the tab.

    Conclusion

    Decision fatigue is real, but choosing doesn't have to be stressful. With fair, cryptographic randomness, support for multiple unique picks, and a session history to fall back on, this decision maker is the easiest way to end the debate - privately and free.
