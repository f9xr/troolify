Purchase Order Maker: Order With Clarity

A purchase order is a buyer formal offer to a supplier. This purchase order maker lists buyer, supplier and ordered items with totals.

What a PO does

It records exactly what is being bought, at what price and quantity, creating a paper trail for procurement and accounts payable.

Where it sits

The flow runs quote to PO to delivery note to invoice. The PO number ties them together for clean reconciliation.

How to use Purchase Order Maker

  1. Enter buyer, supplier and PO number.
  2. List items with quantity and price.
  3. Copy or print the order.
