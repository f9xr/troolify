Unit Converter: Five Essential Converter Kits in One Tab

Whether you are following a recipe, planning a trip, sizing a project or uploading files, unit conversions slow you down when you have to hunt for the right one. This single tool covers length, weight, temperature, volume and digital data - and computes everything locally in your browser.

How the Converter Works

Each category defines every unit by a fixed factor to one base unit - meters for length, grams for weight, milliliters for volume and bytes for data. Your value is converted to the base unit, then scaled to the unit you selected, so any combination of units converts correctly. Temperature is the exception: it uses dedicated formulas (Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin) because temperature scales are offset as well as scaled.

How to Use the Unit Converter

  1. Pick a category tab - Length, Weight, Temperature, Volume or Data.
  2. Choose your "from" and "to" units in the two dropdowns.
  3. Enter a value - the result appears instantly. Use the Swap button to flip the units.

Cooking and Household Conversions

In the Volume tab, teaspoons, tablespoons, cups and fluid ounces use standard US customary measures (1 cup = 236.6 ml). In the Length tab you can switch between millimetres and inches for woodworking or sewing, and the Weight tab easily converts grams to ounces or pounds for recipes and portions. Remember that volume-to-weight conversions differ by ingredient, though - 1 cup of flour and 1 cup of water do not weigh the same.

Digital Data: Decimal vs Binary

The Data tab uses the decimal (SI) convention where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes, matching how storage devices are labelled. If your operating system reports space in binary units (1,024 bytes per KB), the numbers you see there may look slightly different - that discrepancy is normal and has been in every computer user's life. Frequently Asked Questions

How does the unit converter work?

Every unit in a category is defined by a fixed conversion factor to a common base unit (meters, grams, milliliters or bytes). The tool converts your value to the base unit, then scales it to the unit you chose - so any combination converts correctly.

Are KB, MB, GB and TB decimal or binary?

This converter uses the decimal (SI) convention: 1 KB = 1,000 bytes, 1 MB = 1,000 KB, and so on, which is how storage manufacturers label drives. Some operating systems count in binary (1 KB = 1,024 bytes); if your OS shows fewer GB than the label says, that is why.

Does the converter handle temperature?

Yes. Temperature is converted with dedicated formulas for Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin, because temperature scales are offset as well as scaled. The tool applies the standard equations C = K - 273.15 and F = C x 9/5 + 32 automatically.

Is this unit converter accurate enough for cooking?

For liquids it uses standard US customary measures (1 cup = 236.6 ml, 1 tbsp = 14.8 ml, 1 tsp = 4.9 ml), which match common kitchen conversions. Note that volume-to-weight conversions vary by ingredient, so cups to grams need the ingredient's density.
