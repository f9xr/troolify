Delivery Challan Generator: Record Dispatches

A delivery challan travels with dispatched goods. This delivery challan generator lists what left, how much, and where it went.

What it records

Sender, receiver, goods and quantities. It is not an invoice; it simply documents the movement of goods.

Proof of delivery

A receiver signature on the challan confirms the goods arrived, useful for disputes and for matching to later invoices.

How to use Delivery Challan Generator

  1. Enter from, to and challan number.
  2. List goods and quantities.
  3. Copy or print the challan.

Tips for getting accurate results

  • Number challans sequentially.
  • Get a receiver signature.
  • Keep a copy with the invoice.

Key takeaways

  • Challans document goods movement.
  • They are not invoices.
  • Signatures prove delivery.

Frequently asked questions

Is a challan taxable?

It is not a tax invoice.

Do I need one per delivery?

Yes, for traceability.

Can it replace an invoice?

No, they serve different roles.

Is my data uploaded?

No, it stays local.
