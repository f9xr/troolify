Concrete Footing Calculator

Strip (continuous) footings carry load-bearing walls and foundation walls across their full length, so their volume is a rectangular prism: run length times width times depth. This calculator estimates both the yardage for ordering and the bag count for smaller sections.

How to Use the Concrete Footing Calculator

  1. Measure the full run - total lineal feet of footing trench.
  2. Enter width and depth - typical 16 in x 12 in.
  3. Add waste - 5% covers trench irregularity.
  4. Calculate - get volume for the order.

Why the Estimate Matters

Foundation footings are typically 12-24 inches wide and 8-16 inches deep, sized by the soil and wall load. Because a strip footing runs the whole perimeter, the linear measurement is usually the hardest to take accurately - walk the full run, corners included, before you calculate.

Contractor Tip

Corner overlap is real: if you measure the two side runs of a trench that join in the corner, you are double counting a small square. Measure to the outside edges to let the calculator absorb it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much concrete is in a 20 ft strip footing?

A 20 ft footing 16 in wide and 12 in deep needs about 2.96 cubic yards (approx. 38 80 lb bags).

Conclusion

Footings must be right - the Concrete Footing Calculator gives you the concrete volume, bags and weight for any strip foundation run.
