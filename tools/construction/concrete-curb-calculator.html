Concrete Curb Calculator

Curbs line driveways, garden beds, parking lots and road edges, and their volume is a simple rectangular cross-section times the run. Enter the length, width and height of the curb section and the calculator returns cubic yards, bag counts and weight for the whole run.

How to Use the Concrete Curb Calculator

  1. Measure the run - total curb length in feet.
  2. Enter cross-section - width and height in inches.
  3. Add waste - raised to 7% for curb work.
  4. Calculate - read volume, bags and weight.

Why the Estimate Matters

Most curb pours are quiet small-quantity or contractor jobs where ordering by the bag is realistic. Knowing the yardage also lets you compare ready-mix delivery versus bag mixing. The slightly higher default waste of 7% reflects the rounded tops and form bleed typical of curb work.

Contractor Tip

Pour curbs as continuous runs where possible - joints every 4-6 feet prevent cracking, but batteries of small isolated pours use far more forms and labor.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much concrete is a 50 ft curb?

A 50 ft run of 6 in x 6 in curb uses about 12.5 cubic feet - roughly 21 80 lb bags with waste.

Conclusion

Plan curb runs with confidence - the Concrete Curb Calculator returns the exact volume, bag and weight numbers in your browser.
