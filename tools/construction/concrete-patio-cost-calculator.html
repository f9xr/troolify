Concrete Patio Cost Calculator

A concrete patio is the cheapest large flat area a homeowner can build, and its price is governed by square footage and finish. This calculator combines your patio size and a local price per square foot into an instant estimate, with the lumber-yard reality check of total cubic yards.

How to Use the Concrete Patio Cost Calculator

  1. Measure the patio - rough dimensions of the final shape.
  2. Set thickness - 4 in standard for that flat area.
  3. Enter your local price - start in the 6-to-12 range.
  4. Calculate - read total cost and yardage.

Why the Estimate Matters

Broom-finish concrete is the budget default; exposed aggregate, stains and stamped patterns push the price toward the top of the range. Because the slab is small, the minimum-truck charge can dominate a small patio - that is why a 120 sq ft patio can cost more per square foot than a 400 sq ft one.

Contractor Tip

Small patios suffer from the minimum-load 'short pour' surcharge more than any other feature - batch-mixed bags often win for anything under about 60 sq ft.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a concrete patio cost?

A 120 sq ft patio at $9 per sq ft runs about $1,080 with broom finish.

Why is a small patio pricey per square foot?

Ready-mix trucks charge a minimum load, so tiny pours pay more per square foot - bags can be smarter under ~60 sq ft.

Conclusion

The Concrete Patio Cost Calculator gives you the total, the range and the yardage for any patio size - computed entirely in your browser.
