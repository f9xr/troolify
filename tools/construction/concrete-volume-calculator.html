Concrete Volume Calculator

Estimating concrete volume is the first step of every pour, whether you are ordering ready-mix by the yard or buying bags at the hardware store. This calculator turns length, width and thickness into cubic feet and cubic yards, then translates the volume into common bag counts so you know exactly what to bring to the site.

How to Use the Concrete Volume Calculator

  1. Measure the pour - length and width in feet, thickness in inches.
  2. Set how many - identical slabs, or leave at 1.
  3. Add waste - 5% is a common allowance for slabs.
  4. Calculate - read volume in cubic feet and yards.
  5. Check bags - match the result to the bag size you plan to mix.

Why the Estimate Matters

Ready-mix plants price by the cubic yard, so knowing your yardage keeps quotes honest and avoids ordering short. Bag buyers get a direct answer on how many 40, 50, 60 or 80 lb sacks to load into the truck. Adding a small waste percentage covers spillage, uneven subgrades and rounding.

Contractor Tip

Always round bag counts up - few things are worse than being a bag short mid-pour, and mixing trips to the store always cost more time than the extra bag costs money.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many cubic feet are in a cubic yard?

There are 27 cubic feet in 1 cubic yard (3 ft x 3 ft x 3 ft).

The Concrete Volume Calculator gives you the yardage, bag count and weight for nearly any pour - all computed privately in your browser with zero uploads.
