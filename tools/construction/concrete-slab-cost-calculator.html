Concrete Slab Cost Calculator

Slabs cover everything from tool sheds to workshop floors, and their cost is area-driven: length times width, times a local price per square foot. This calculator returns the estimated total along with the cubic yards of concrete needed, so you can cross-check a quote against both the square footage and the truck volumes.

How to Use the Concrete Slab Cost Calculator

  1. Measure the slab - length and width at the edges of the form.
  2. Choose a thickness - 4 in standard, 5-6 in for vehicles.
  3. Enter the local price - start at the 6-to-12 dollar benchmark.
  4. Calculate - read the total and the yardage.

Why the Estimate Matters

Because a slab is flat, thickness barely changes the square-foot price but does drive the yardage - a 4-in slab uses 1 cubic yard per 81 sq ft. Comparing quotes by price per square foot is the fastest honesty check, and finishing options like broom, trowel or colored oxide each move the price a little higher.

Contractor Tip

Ask for the price 'in place' - poured, finished and cured - not just the ready-mix. The truck is often the smallest line on a proper slab quote.
