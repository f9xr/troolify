Concrete Slab Quote Calculator

A concrete slab quote is easier to compare when it is split into labor, materials and site prep - so you can see which contractor is charging for what. This calculator builds that breakdown from your slab area and a total price per square foot, then normalizes the percentages and shows the yardage that should sit behind the material line.

How to Use the Concrete Slab Quote Calculator

  1. Measure the slab - length and width of the final pour.
  2. Enter the total price - what the contractor quoted per square foot.
  3. Tune the split - labor, materials and prep percentages.
  4. Calculate - read the dollar breakdown and the yardage.

Why the Estimate Matters

Labor is the biggest single line on most flatwork, since forming, placing and finishing are skilled and time-bound before the concrete sets. Materials include the ready-mix truck and any reinforcement; site prep covers grading, forms and cleanup. Forcing a quote into these three buckets is the fastest way to spot double-charging or a suspiciously cheap material line.

Contractor Tip

If a quote will not split into labor, materials and prep, ask politely - contractors who cannot explain their own price usually cannot control it either.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should a concrete slab quote include?

Labor, ready-mix materials and delivery, site prep and clean-up - plus any joints or reinforcement as separate line items.

Do I need the yardage on a quote?

Yes - check the material line against the cubic yards the slab needs; it is the quickest way to catch an over-ordered (and over-billed) truck.

Conclusion

The Concrete Slab Quote Calculator turns a flat per-square-foot price into a transparent labor, materials and prep breakdown - privately, in your browser.
