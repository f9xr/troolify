Concrete Steps Calculator

Concrete steps are estimated by viewing the staircase in profile - the rise and run build a triangle whose area, times the stair width, gives the volume. This calculator walks that shape for any rise, tread and width, returning cubic yards, bags and weight.

How to Use the Concrete Steps Calculator

  1. Count the steps - number of risers.
  2. Enter rise and tread - in inches each.
  3. Enter stair width - in feet.
  4. Calculate - read volume, bags and weight.

Why the Estimate Matters

Because the riser face is mostly vertical fill beneath the treads, the triangle method gives the honest core volume. Stairsteps with landings or flared bases add material, so this tool is the conservative starting point for a quote.

Contractor Tip

The 7-11 rule - 7 inch rise, 11 inch tread - is the comfortable comfort zone for exterior stairs. Whatever you choose, keep it identical on every step.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much concrete is a 4-step entrance?

Four steps at 7 in rise, 12 in tread and 4 ft wide take about 2.1 cubic feet per step - roughly 0.31 cubic yards total before waste.

Does the calculator include landings?

No - it models the step runs. Add any landing slabs to the total separately.

Conclusion

Entrance stairs and porches both follow the profile-triangle rule - this calculator renders the volume, bags and weight in your browser.
