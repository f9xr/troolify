Garage Floor Calculator

A garage floor is a thick, vehicle-rated slab, and its volume follows the standard slab formula: length times width times thickness. A standard 24 by 24 foot two-car garage at 6 inches thick takes about 10.6 cubic yards - easily a three-truck order.

How to Use the Garage Floor Calculator

  1. Enter garage length and width - inside or outside of walls consistently.
  2. Enter intended thickness - 6 in is the standard.
  3. Add waste - 5% is typical.
  4. Calculate - read cubic yards for ordering.

Why the Estimate Matters

Garage slabs run thicker than patios because they carry parked vehicles and often surface-mounted storage. The calculator is set to a 6-inch default for exactly that reason. Because this is a near-worst-case pour for waste - chutes, door aprons and slope control - the waste slider matters here.

Contractor Tip

Garage floors should slope slightly to the door for drainage - even an eighth-inch-per-foot fall must be baked into the screed plan before the truck arrives.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many yards of concrete for a garage floor?

A 24x24 ft garage at 6 inches thick needs about 10.6 cubic yards (with 5% waste).

Conclusion

Workshops, garages and outbuildings all share the same slab math - get the yardage, bags and weight from this calculator, in your browser.
