Timesheet Calculator: Total a Week of Hours in Seconds

Adding up a week row by row is exactly the kind of arithmetic that invites mistakes. This timesheet calculator takes a daily start, end and break for each day, shows the hours for that day instantly, and rolls them into a weekly total with any overtime highlighted.

Daily rows, one running total

Each row behaves like the single hours calculator: it converts the times to minutes, handles an overnight crossover, and removes the break. Days with no times are skipped, so a part-time week works fine. The weekly total is shown both as hours and minutes and as decimal hours, and anything past forty hours is reported separately as overtime, a figure many US payroll rules use as a threshold.

Where it saves time

Employees can pre-check a timesheet before submitting it, managers can verify a claimed week quickly, and freelancers tracking billable days get a clean total to invoice against. It is also useful for logging study hours, training time or the hours spent on a personal project.

How to use Timesheet Calculator

  1. Enter a start, end and break for each day you worked.
  2. Watch the daily hours appear as you type.
  3. Press Add up the week for the totals.

Tips for getting accurate results

  • Leave a day blank if you did not work it - blank rows are ignored.
  • Breaks are entered in minutes, so a 45-minute lunch is 45.
  • The overtime figure uses a 40-hour weekly threshold.

Key takeaways

Frequently asked questions

How is overtime worked out?

Any weekly total above forty hours is shown separately as overtime, using a common 40-hour threshold.

Can I leave days empty?

Yes. Days with no start or end time are simply skipped in the total.

Does it handle night shifts?

Yes. If an end time is earlier than its start, that day is treated as crossing midnight.

Do breaks need to be in minutes?

Yes - enter 30 for a half-hour break, or 0 for no break.

Is my timesheet saved anywhere?

No. All values stay in your browser and are cleared when you leave the page.

Conclusion

Get the week right the first time. The timesheet calculator totals your hours and flags overtime automatically. For a single shift, use the hours calculator.
