Business Days Calculator: Count Work Days, Not Calendar Days

A deadline that sounds generous on the calendar often is not once you strip out weekends. This business days calculator counts the working days between two dates, excludes Saturdays and Sundays, and lets you remove public holidays so the number you get matches the days people are actually at their desks.

What counts as a business day

A business day is any Monday to Friday that is not a holiday you have listed. The tool walks from your start date to your end date one day at a time, skipping weekends and any date in your holiday box. You decide whether the end date itself is part of the span - useful when a deadline falls on the closing day. It prints working days, weekend days and the raw total side by side so the difference is obvious.

Where an exact count matters

Contract and legal clauses often promise delivery in a number of business days, not calendar days. Support teams quote response windows the same way. Project managers use the figure to see how much real working time sits between today and a launch, and freelancers use it to set turnaround times they can actually meet once weekends are removed.

How to use Business Days Calculator

  1. Enter a start and an end date.
  2. Choose whether to include the end date.
  3. Paste any holidays to exclude, one date per line.
  4. Press Count business days.

Tips for getting accurate results

  • List regional holidays that apply to the people doing the work, not just national ones.
  • If a deadline is inclusive of the final day, set include end date to Yes.
  • For anything crossing a year boundary, add next year holidays too.

Key takeaways

  • Weekends are removed automatically.
  • Any holiday list you enter is subtracted.
  • You control whether the end date counts.

Frequently asked questions

Does it exclude weekends automatically?

Yes. Saturdays and Sundays are never counted as business days.

Can I remove public holidays?

Yes. Enter one date per line in YYYY-MM-DD form and each is excluded from the count.

Is the end date included?

That is up to you - switch include end date on or off depending on how your deadline is worded.

Does it handle dates in reverse order?

Yes. If the end is before the start, the two are swapped so the count is still correct.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser.
