Week Number Calculator: ISO Weeks Without the Spreadsheet

Planners, payroll weeks and project schedules all lean on week numbers, yet working them out by hand is fiddly because ISO weeks do not line up neatly with January 1. This week number calculator finds the ISO week for any date and also runs the other way, turning a year and week into exact dates.

Why ISO weeks are odd at the edges

Under the ISO 8601 standard a week always begins on Monday, and week 1 is the week that contains the first Thursday of the year. That means the first day or two of January can belong to the last week of the previous year, and the final days of December can fall into week 1 of the next. The tool applies that rule precisely, so early January and late December dates report the week year you would see in a proper planner.

Working with week numbers

Manufacturing and project teams often schedule by week, German and Nordic calendars print week numbers in the margin, and payroll systems may run on weekly periods. Entering a date gives you the week number for planning; entering a year and week gives the Monday to Sunday span, which is what you need to book a slot or set a reminder.

How to use Week Number Calculator

  1. Choose whether to convert a date or a week.
  2. Enter the date, or the year and week number.
  3. Press Calculate week to see the week number and its start and end dates.

Frequently asked questions

Which week numbering standard is used?

ISO 8601, the international standard where weeks start on Monday and week 1 contains the first Thursday.

Why is my January date in the previous year week?

Under ISO rules the start of January can fall into the last week of the previous year; the week year shown reflects that.

Can I find the dates for a week number?

Yes. Switch to year and week mode and enter both values to get the Monday to Sunday span.

Does it show the current week number?

Yes - press Sample to load today, and the week number appears immediately.

Is anything stored?

No. All calculations happen locally in your browser.
