Pace Calculator: Turn a Time Into a Pace and Speed

Runners, cyclists and swimmers talk in paces even when they think in finish times. This pace calculator takes a distance and a time and returns your pace per kilometre or mile, your average speed, and the equivalent pace in the other unit.

Pace, speed and the difference

Pace is time per unit of distance - usually minutes per kilometre or per mile - while speed is distance per unit of time. They are reciprocals, so a faster speed means a smaller pace number. The tool divides your total seconds by the distance for pace, and the distance by the time in hours for speed, then converts across kilometres and miles with the standard 1.609344 factor.

Training with a pace

A pace figure is easier to hold than a finish time because you can check it every kilometre. Runners use it to plan even splits and goal times, cyclists and swimmers use the same idea in their own units, and anyone following a programme can see whether a run was on target. Comparing the equivalent pace across units helps when a plan or a watch reports the other system.

How to use Pace Calculator

  1. Enter the distance and choose kilometres or miles.
  2. Enter the time as hours, minutes and seconds.
  3. Press Calculate pace for your pace and speed.

  • Use the equivalent pace when a plan is written in the other unit.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between pace and speed?

Pace is time per distance (minutes per km or mile); speed is distance per time (km/h or mph).

Can I use miles instead of kilometres?

Yes. Choose miles and the pace is given per mile and the speed in mph.

How is the equivalent pace calculated?

It multiplies or divides by 1.609344, the number of kilometres in a mile.

Is this only for runners?

No. Cyclists, swimmers and walkers can use the same pace and speed maths.

Do you save my times?

No. All calculations happen in your browser.

Conclusion

Know your pace before the next session. The pace calculator turns distance and time into a pace and speed you can train by. For elapsed time alone, try the time duration calculator.
