Study Break Timer: Focus with the Pomodoro Method

Studying in focused bursts keeps attention sharp. This study break timer alternates work blocks and rest following the Pomodoro pattern.

The Pomodoro method

Work in a focused block (typically 25 minutes), take a short break, and after four cycles take a longer rest. The rhythm limits fatigue and protects concentration.

Tuning the intervals

Some people prefer 50-minute blocks with 10-minute breaks. Adjust the fields to match your attention span.

How to use Study Break Timer

  1. Set study and break lengths.
  2. Press Start to begin the first block.
  3. Follow the phases or reset anytime.
