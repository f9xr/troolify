Time Duration Calculator: Add Up Hours and Minutes the Easy Way

Adding shifts by hand is fiddly - 9:47 to 17:23 is not a quick sum. This calculator converts times to minutes, handles overnight shifts automatically, and returns the duration in hours and minutes plus the decimal hours you need for timesheets and payroll.

What the Duration Calculator Shows

  • Total duration - the exact hours and minutes between the times.
  • Decimal hours - e.g. 7.5 for 7h 30m, the format most payroll systems expect.
  • Total minutes - useful for quick cross-checks.
  • Net worked time - the gross duration minus any break you enter.

How to Use the Calculator

  1. Set the start and end times - both as clock times, e.g. 09:00 and 17:00.
  2. Add a break if needed, using hours and minutes separately (e.g. 0 hours, 30 minutes).
  3. Use "Use current time as end" to stamp the live clock as the end time.

The result updates instantly and shows both the gross duration and the net duration after the break.

Overnight Shifts Work Automatically

When the end time is earlier than the start time - for example 22:00 to 06:00 - the calculator detects the overnight case and adds 24 hours, so it returns 8 hours instead of a confusing negative number. A small note appears on the result to confirm the overnight path was used.

Why Decimal Hours Matter

Timesheets and many payroll tools quote hours as decimals: 7.5 hours rather than 7h 30m. Tracking your shift with our Sleep Time Calculator as well keeps work and rest schedules consistent, and our Unix Timestamp Converter helps developers convert time-of-day to epoch values for automation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate the hours between two times?

Enter a start and an end time and the tool converts both to minutes past midnight, then subtracts. If the end time is earlier than the start time, 24 hours is added automatically, meaning overnight shifts are handled correctly. Break minutes (if any) are then deducted from the gross duration.

What is 7.5 hours in hours and minutes?

7.5 decimal hours equals 7 hours and 30 minutes. To get minutes from a decimal, multiply the fractional part by 60. This calculator shows the duration in both formats side by side so you never have to convert manually.

Does the time duration calculator handle overnight shifts?

Yes. Whenever the end time is earlier than the start time, the calculator detects the overnight case and adds 24 hours, so a shift from 22:00 to 06:00 correctly returns 8 hours rather than a negative duration.

Is the Troolify time duration calculator safe to use?

Yes. Every calculation runs locally inside your browser tab. Your times are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted to a server, so your schedule details stay completely private.
