Add to a Date Calculator: Jump Forward on the Calendar Instantly

Planning a deadline, a travel itinerary or simply curious about what day of the week a future date falls on? This calculator lets you add any number of days, weeks, months or years to a starting date and get an accurate result immediately - no spreadsheets, no calendar counting, no sign-up.

How It Works

Select a start date using the date picker, type a number in the amount field, and pick the unit you want to add. The result appears instantly: the new date, its weekday, and both ISO and long-form representations. You can also enter a negative number to go backwards in time.

Month and Year Rollover

The calculator handles end-of-month edge cases correctly. Adding one month to January 31 gives you February 28 (or 29 in a leap year), because February never has 31 days. Adding one year to February 29 in a leap year correctly rolls to February 28 in a non-leap year. These clamping rules match what you would see in Google Calendar or Outlook.

Practical Use Cases

Date addition comes up more often than you might think. Subscription renewals, visa expiry windows, pregnancy due dates, product launch timelines and school term planning all rely on adding intervals to a known start date. Rather than manually counting on a wall calendar, let the math happen automatically.

How to Use the Calculator

  1. Pick a start date by clicking the date field or typing directly.
  2. Enter an amount - it can be any whole number, including negatives.
  3. Choose the unit from the dropdown: days, weeks, months or years.
  4. Read the result below, and copy it with one click if needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when I add a month to January 31?

When you add one month to January 31, the calculator correctly rolls into February and clamps to the last valid day of that month. In a non-leap year the result is February 28; in a leap year it is February 29. This clamping behaviour prevents impossible dates and matches what calendar applications show.

Do weekends or holidays affect the result?

No. The calculator works strictly with calendar dates, so weekends and public holidays are counted exactly the same as weekdays. If you need to skip non-working days you would need a business-day calculator, which is a separate concept.

Can I subtract by entering a negative amount?

Yes. Enter a negative number such as -7 to go backwards in time. This makes the tool flexible for both addition and subtraction without switching modes. For a dedicated subtraction experience see the companion Subtract from a Date Calculator.

Is my date data saved or sent anywhere?

Absolutely not. All calculations run inside your browser using plain JavaScript. No date value is transmitted to any server, stored in a database or logged in any way. When you close the tab everything is gone.
