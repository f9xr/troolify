Camera Recorder - Record Webcam Video in Your Browser

Record your webcam in the browser with MediaRecorder and download the WebM file.

How the recorder works

The page opens your webcam with getUserMedia and records the stream with the MediaRecorder API - the same primitives used by meeting apps. Everything runs in this tab: frames never touch a server, so the recording is fully private.

Output format

Recordings are saved as WebM (VP9 or VP8 depending on browser support), which modern editors, ffmpeg and social platforms accept. The microphone is optional - enable the checkbox before starting the camera if you want audio.

How to use Camera Recorder

  1. Click Start camera and allow the permission prompt
  2. Click Record; the red REC badge shows capture is live
  3. Click Stop, then Download the WebM clip

Tips for getting accurate results

  • Camera access requires a secure context (https:// or localhost)
  • The microphone toggle only applies when the camera is (re)started
  • Clips stay in memory for this tab session - download what you need before leaving

Frequently asked questions

Is the video uploaded?

No. MediaRecorder produces a local blob you download yourself.

Why WebM and not MP4?

WebM is the only format browsers can record natively; convert with any video editor if you need MP4.
