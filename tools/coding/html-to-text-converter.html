HTML to Text Converter: Extract Clean Content in One Click

Newsletters, scraped pages, RSS feeds and CMS exports all arrive wrapped in markup. This converter turns that HTML back into clean, readable text - and with the right mode selected it will even keep your links and heading structure for you.

About the Tool

The converter parses your HTML with the browser's built-in DOM parser, so tag handling and entity decoding are always exact. Script and style blocks are stripped immediately, and pure formatting tags leave nothing behind but their content.

Three modes cover the common workflows: plain text for emails and notes, a link mode that renders anchors as [text](url), and a heading mode that keeps h1-h6 levels as #, ##, ### prefixes so the document outline survives.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Paste your HTML into the input area - the extraction runs live as you type.
  2. Choose an output mode - plain text, keep links, or keep heading prefixes.
  3. Copy the result with one click and paste it into your email, word processor, or content store.

Because everything happens on your device there is no upload delay, no size limit beyond your browser's textarea, and no copy of your markup stored anywhere.

Why It Matters

Manually deleting <div>, <span> and inline styles from a large block is slow and error-prone, especially when the source is minified. A reliable converter gives you exact plain text every time - ideal for repurposing content, building datasets, or reading a page without its styling.

Pair it with the Markdown to HTML Converter to reverse the flow, or run the output through the Word Counter after extraction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the converter keep link URLs when extracting text?

Yes. In link mode every anchor is rendered as [link text](url), so you keep both the visible words and the destination. Switch to plain mode and links collapse to just their text.

How are HTML entities in the source handled?

They are decoded automatically. The tool parses the markup with the browser's DOM parser, so entities like &lt; or &copy; come out as their real characters in the extracted text.

Is the Troolify version safe for my data?

Yes. Everything runs locally inside your browser tab - nothing you paste or upload is sent to a server, so drafts and data stay private.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Like all Troolify tools this runs on desktop and mobile browsers with no install and no account required.

Conclusion

Whether you are cleaning up an email export or rebuilding content from a scraped page, this converter strips the markup and hands you text you can actually use - quickly, accurately, and without your HTML ever leaving the browser.
