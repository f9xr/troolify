AWS IAM Policy Analyzer: Catch Over-Permission Before It Ships

An IAM policy is easy to write and hard to review. A single * can hand a service every permission in your account. This AWS IAM policy analyzer reads a policy document and points out the wildcards, public principals and escalation patterns that turn a convenience into an incident.

What the analyzer flags

The tool rates each finding by severity. Critical issues include Action:* and a public Principal:*. High-severity findings cover service-wide wildcards on sensitive services, Resource:* with broad actions, and iam:PassRole without a condition. Medium findings note missing conditions and the fragile NotAction pattern.

A starting point, not a verdict

This is a static, heuristic review. It cannot know your intent or the resources behind an ARN, so treat each finding as a question to answer rather than a guaranteed bug. It is the fastest way to catch the obvious mistakes before a deeper, tool-assisted audit.

How to use AWS IAM Policy Analyzer

  1. Paste the full policy document as JSON.
  2. Read the severity summary for the shape of the problem.
  3. Work from critical findings down, applying the suggested fix.
  4. Re-run the tool to confirm the wildcards are gone.

Tips for getting accurate results

  • Prefer explicit action lists over service wildcards for sensitive services.
  • Add conditions such as MFA or source IP to narrow broadly-scoped statements.
  • Never combine Principal:* with Effect:Allow.

Key takeaways

  • Wildcards on sensitive services are the highest-value fix.
  • PassRole without conditions is a common escalation path.
  • A heuristic pass catches the obvious problems quickly.

Frequently asked questions

Does it validate my whole AWS account?

No. It analyses the single policy document you paste. For account-wide analysis use a dedicated scanner.

Why is Resource:* only sometimes critical?

It depends on the actions. Widening a read on one service is less severe than combining it with Action:* or write access.

What is iam:PassRole risk?

Granting PassRole lets a principal attach roles to services, which can escalate privileges if those roles are powerful.

Is my policy uploaded?

No. The analysis runs entirely in your browser.

Do I still need a full audit?

Yes. Use this as a fast first pass, then validate against your real requirements and tools.

Conclusion

Give a policy the least privilege it needs and it stops being a liability. This AWS IAM policy analyzer surfaces the wildcards worth fixing first. For related checks, try our IP allowlist generator.
