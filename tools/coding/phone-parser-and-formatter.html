Phone Parser & Formatter - Parse & Format Phone Numbers

Parse any phone number into E.164, international and national formats, with country detection.

What this phone parser does

Paste any phone number - with spaces, dashes, brackets or a country prefix - and get back the standard formats developers need: E.164 for APIs, an international display format, and a readable national format. Country detection covers 48 common calling codes.

Formats explained

E.164 (+442079460958) is the format SMS and voice APIs expect. International adds spacing for humans. National drops the country code and applies local grouping, e.g. (020) 7946 0958.

How to use Phone Parser & Formatter

  1. Type or paste a phone number
  2. Click Parse (results also update as you type)
  3. Click any result tile to copy that format

Tips for getting accurate results

  • Include the country prefix when you know it - local numbers are parsed as domestic
  • E.164 is the safest format to store in a database
  • Detection is heuristic; always let users confirm the country

Frequently asked questions

Does this validate the number?

It checks structure and detects the country, but cannot confirm the number is assigned.

Is my input sent anywhere?

No. Parsing runs entirely in your browser.
