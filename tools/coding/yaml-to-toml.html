YAML to TOML Converter: Re-Indent Your Infrastructure

YAML is famously flexible — sometimes too flexible. This YAML to TOML converter parses the common YAML subset (mappings, sequences, inline arrays and maps, block scalars) and re-emits it as strictly-typed TOML with [tables] and [[array of tables]].

The conversion happens in your browser with nothing uploaded.

How the structure is mapped

A YAML mapping becomes TOML key/value pairs, and a nested mapping becomes a [table] section. A sequence of scalars stays an inline array, while a sequence of mappings becomes repeated [[name]] blocks, and inline flow forms such as [a, b] or {x: 1} are parsed too.

Block scalars and typed values

Literal (|) and folded (>) block scalars are folded into single TOML strings, with newlines escaped inside the quotes. Numbers, booleans and nulls keep their types; quoted strings stay quoted.

A pragmatic subset

The parser covers the YAML used in real configs: indentation-based nesting, comments, quoted keys, anchors and tags are not interpreted. If your document uses advanced features, convert the plain parts and validate the result with a TOML parser.

How to use YAML to TOML

  1. Paste a YAML document (compose file, app config, pipeline) into the input box.
  2. The TOML appears live as you type, or press Convert.
  3. Review the typed output and copy it to your TOML-native service.

Tips for getting accurate results

  • Use the sample to see how sequences of mappings become [[array of tables]].
  • Keys containing dots or spaces are quoted automatically.
  • Block scalars (| and >) are supported, but JSON-style flow scalars with weird characters should be quoted in the source.

Frequently asked questions

Which YAML features are supported?

Indentation-based mappings and sequences, inline arrays and maps, quoted strings, comments and | / > block scalars. Anchors, aliases and tags are not interpreted.

How do I convert a sequence of mappings?

It becomes repeated [[name]] blocks, one per item, which is the idiomatic TOML equivalent.

Are YAML comments kept?

No — comments are stripped. Only data is converted.

What happens to YAML dates?

They are kept as strings, exactly as written, so no timezone is introduced.

Where does the conversion run?

100% in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
