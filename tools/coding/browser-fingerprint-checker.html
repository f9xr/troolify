Browser Fingerprint Checker: Understand Tracking

Websites can identify you without cookies using fingerprints - combinations of your screen, language, hardware and more. This browser fingerprint checker shows those signals.

What makes a fingerprint

Screen size, timezone, installed fonts, language list, CPU cores and touch support combine into a surprisingly unique signature.

Reducing exposure

Privacy-focused browsers normalise or randomise these signals. Blocking trackers and using a standard browser profile narrows your fingerprint.

How to use Browser Fingerprint Checker

  1. Press Analyse browser.
  2. Review the signal list.
  3. Note the fingerprint hash.
