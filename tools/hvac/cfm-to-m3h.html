CFM to m3/h

Ready - airflow conversion in this tab.

Conversion is dimensional only - it does not alter the airflow basis, so do not re-apply the conversion when a system mixes units for the same stream. Full disclaimer.

An exact conversion

One cubic foot per minute equals 0.02831685 m³/min, so multiplying by 60 gives 1.6990108 m³/h. There is no rounding to fight - the same airflow is simply expressed in a different unit.

Keep the design basis

When a system mixes units - a North American fan datasheet feeding a European coil section - convert once and label the result. Converting the same stream twice is the classic error that silently doubles or halves the design airflow.

L/s for small streams

European grilles and diffusers are commonly rated in litres per second. The converter reports L/s and m³/s alongside m³/h so grille selections stay on one basis.

Reverse direction

Need the other direction? Use the m3/h to CFM tool - the factor inverts cleanly at 0.5885778.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 1000 CFM in m3/h?

1,699.01 m³/h exactly (1000 × 1.6990108).

Is CFM larger or smaller than m3/h?

Numerically larger - CFM counts per minute while m³/h counts per hour, so CFM × 60 (and × 0.0283) yields m³/h.

Does the conversion account for air density?

No - it converts volume flow as measured. Mass flow and density corrections are separate calculations for altitude and temperature.

When should I use L/s instead?

For grilles, diffusers and smaller ventilation rates on metric projects. Keep the whole branch of the system on a single unit.

Conclusion

Airflow units convert cleanly; the discipline is converting once and never re-scaling a number that is already in its intended unit.
