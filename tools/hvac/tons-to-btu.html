Nominal Tons to BTU/h

Ready - tons to BTU/h conversion in this tab.

Nominal tons convert cleanly to BTU/h; delivered capacity still depends on the ratings curve and operating conditions of the specific equipment. Full disclaimer.

The conversion itself

Multiplying nominal tons by 12,000 gives BTU/h - a 3-ton system is 36,000 BTU/h; a 1.5-ton system is 18,000 and a 5-ton is 60,000. The same figure in thermal kilowatts is tons multiplied by 3.51685.

Why nominal is not delivered

Rated capacity is published at a standard condition (typically 95 F outdoor air). On a hotter design day, the same tonnage delivers less. Converting tons is about comparison and bracketing, while equipment selection uses the published curve at your design condition.

Standard brackets

Equipment steps in half-ton increments: 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 3.5, 4, and 5 tons are the everyday residential range. If a design load converts to 2.6 tons, the practical choices are 2.5 or 3 - and the decision should consider the fan and capacity-curve details, not just the arithmetic.

Checking a nameplate

When a quote says "3 tons" but the brochure lists 34,000 BTU/h, you have nominal vs rated mismatch. Normalize with this tool, then use the BTU per Square Foot check to see whether the stated capacity fits the footprint.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many BTU/h is a 2-ton AC?

24,000 BTU/h - exactly 12,000 per ton. A 1.5-ton unit is 18,000 and a 3-ton is 36,000.

What is the kW of 1 ton of cooling?

3.51685 kW thermal. The electrical power drawn is lower and depends on the unit's energy efficiency ratio.

Could a 4-ton unit deliver only 3-tons of work?

Yes, off the rating point. Hot ambients, undersized lines, filter drag and low charge all reduce delivered capacity below nominal.

Is tonnage rounded up or down?

Match to the closest standard bracket but verify with the manufacturer curve. If you are consistently 20% above a bracket, check the underlying load rather than buying bigger.

Conclusion

Tons, BTU/h and kilowatts are the same capacity in three currencies. Convert cleanly, then source the actual curves before buying.
