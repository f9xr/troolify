Convert Word to Text: Extract Clean Plain Text from Word Documents

Microsoft Word documents carry a lot of hidden formatting, styles and metadata. When you need just the text, copying from Word often leaves you with extra line breaks, invisible characters and inconsistent spacing. This free converter strips everything away and gives you clean, readable plain text with paragraph breaks intact.

About the Tool

The tool works in two modes. Paste directly from Word using Ctrl+V and the contenteditable area captures the HTML from the clipboard. Or toggle the source input and paste raw Word HTML yourself. The cleaning engine uses the browser's native DOMParser to strip Word-specific markup, then extracts textContent to get the pure text.

Paragraph breaks are preserved by inserting double newlines between paragraph elements, headings and list items, then collapsing any sequences of three or more blank lines down to two.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Paste from Word into the contenteditable area, or toggle the source input for raw HTML.
  2. Click Convert to strip all formatting and extract plain text.
  3. Copy the result with one click.

Why It Matters

Whether you are migrating content to a plain-text system, preparing copy for email or social media, or cleaning up client-provided documents, this tool saves you from the tedious work of manual cleanup. It handles nested lists, tables and heading structures automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I paste directly from Microsoft Word?

Yes. Paste with Ctrl+V into the contenteditable area. The tool captures the HTML from the clipboard and strips all formatting to plain text.

How are paragraph breaks preserved?

The tool inserts double newlines between paragraphs, headings and list items, then collapses any extra blank lines for clean output.

Will my content be uploaded?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent to any server.

Does it handle tables and lists?

The HTML cleaning step removes table markup but preserves list content. List items get line breaks between them.

Conclusion

This converter turns messy Word documents into clean plain text in seconds. Every operation runs locally in your browser, so sensitive content like contracts, invoices or personal notes never leaves your device.
