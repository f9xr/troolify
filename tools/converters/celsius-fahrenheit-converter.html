Celsius to Fahrenheit Converter: The Only Formula You Need

Most of the world measures temperature in Celsius while the United States, Liberia and Myanmar use Fahrenheit. The conversion between them is one line of arithmetic, and once you have it you never need to look it up again. This converter shows the working every time so the formula sticks.

  3. Read the reference points below the result to sanity-check your figure against things you already know.

The Two Formulas

  • Celsius to Fahrenheit: multiply by 1.8 (or 9/5) and add 32.
  • Fahrenheit to Celsius: subtract 32, then divide by 1.8 (or multiply by 5/9).

You will see the 1.8 written as 9/5 in most textbooks. They are the same number, and the fraction avoids rounding, so use whichever form you remember. A quick mental check that catches most mistakes: 0 degrees Celsius is 32 degrees Fahrenheit, and 100 Celsius is 212 Fahrenheit.

Why Two Scales?

The Celsius scale was built around the freezing and boiling points of water, which is why 0 and 100 are the anchors and a degree is a nice round fraction of that range. Fahrenheit came first and used a finer scale anchored on the body and freezing brine, so its degrees are smaller and the numbers less convenient. Neither is more accurate - they simply describe the same physical change with different-sized steps, which is exactly why an offset of 32 is needed as well as a scale factor.

Everyday Temperatures Worth Knowing

Water freezing is 0 Celsius and 32 Fahrenheit, boiling water is 100 and 212, normal body temperature is about 37 and 98.6, and a typical indoor room sits around 20 Celsius or 68 Fahrenheit. If a converted figure looks strange, check it against one of these before trusting it - the converter cannot know whether you meant Celsius or Fahrenheit in the first place.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply the Celsius value by 1.8, then add 32. For 100 degrees Celsius that is 100 × 1.8 + 32 = 212 degrees Fahrenheit, which is why water boils at 212 F.

How do you convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?

Subtract 32 from the Fahrenheit value, then divide by 1.8. For 212 F that is (212 - 32) / 1.8 = 100 degrees Celsius.

What are the key temperature points?

Water freezes at 0 degrees Celsius and 32 degrees Fahrenheit, and boils at 100 Celsius and 212 Fahrenheit. Normal human body temperature is about 37 Celsius or 98.6 Fahrenheit, and a comfortable room is roughly 20 Celsius or 68 Fahrenheit.
