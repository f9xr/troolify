Convert Plain Text to HTML: Instant Markup from Raw Text

Plain text is the universal format, but the web runs on HTML. This free converter bridges the gap, turning your raw text into clean, structured HTML with paragraphs, lists and links detected automatically. Paste your text, adjust the options and get ready-to-use markup in seconds.

About the Tool

The converter splits your input on blank lines to detect paragraphs, then checks each line for common list markers: dashes, asterisks and numbered prefixes like "1." "2." "3." URLs are found with a robust regex and wrapped in anchor tags. Special characters can be escaped to their HTML entities for safe embedding in existing pages.

A live preview shows the rendered output below the HTML, so you can verify the result before copying.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Paste your text into the input area.
  2. Toggle options: escape characters, convert URLs, detect bullet or numbered lists.
  3. Click Convert and copy the generated HTML.
  4. Preview the rendered output in the live preview area.

Why It Matters

Whether you are pasting a blog draft, formatting email content, or building a CMS entry from a text file, this tool saves you from writing HTML by hand. The auto-detection means you get sensible output without any manual markup.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the tool detect paragraphs?

Blank lines in your input are treated as paragraph separators. Each block of text between blank lines is wrapped in <p> tags.

Can it convert URLs in text to clickable links?

Yes. With the "Convert URLs to links" checkbox enabled, any URL in your text is automatically wrapped in an anchor tag with target=_blank.

What does "Escape special characters" do?

It converts characters like < > and & to their HTML entities so they display as literal text rather than being interpreted as HTML markup.

Will my text be uploaded to a server?

No. All conversion happens locally in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server.

Conclusion

This converter takes the pain out of turning plain text into HTML. With paragraph detection, list auto-formatting and URL linking, you get clean markup ready for any web page, email or CMS, all processed privately in your browser.
