Convert Email to HTML: Build Inline-Styled Emails from Plain Text

Crafting HTML emails that render correctly across dozens of email clients is notoriously difficult. Most email clients ignore external stylesheets and even embedded <style> blocks. This free converter takes the pain out of the process by generating a complete, inline-styled HTML email from your plain text, complete with subject header, sender branding and an optional call-to-action button.

About the Tool

The converter builds a table-based HTML email using only inline styles, ensuring compatibility with Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, Yahoo Mail and other popular clients. The email body is split on blank lines into paragraphs, all user input is escaped to prevent injection, and the output includes a subtle footer with "Sent via Troolify".

The optional call-to-action button is rendered as an anchor tag with inline styling, so it works even in email clients that do not support CSS buttons.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Paste your email body into the text area.
  2. Add a subject and sender name in the optional fields.
  3. Enable the CTA button and provide a label and URL if needed.
  4. Click Convert, then copy the HTML or download the .html file.

Why It Matters

Whether you are sending a newsletter, transactional email or marketing campaign, you need HTML that renders everywhere. By generating fully inline-styled markup, this tool eliminates the guesswork and gives you email-ready HTML in seconds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why use inline styles for email HTML?

Many email clients strip <style> blocks and external stylesheets. Inline styles are the most reliable way to ensure your email renders correctly across Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail and other clients.

Can I download the generated HTML?

Yes. Click the Download .html button to save the complete email HTML file to your device.

Is my email content uploaded?

No. All generation happens locally in your browser. Your email text, subject and any other inputs never leave your device.

What email clients does this work with?

The generated HTML uses widely-supported inline styles and table-based layout, making it compatible with Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, Yahoo Mail and most other email clients.

Conclusion

This converter turns plain text into production-ready HTML email in seconds. With inline styling, CTA button support and a one-click download, it is the fastest way to go from draft to inbox-ready, all without leaving your browser.
