Number to Words Converter: Spell Out Amounts

Cheques, contracts and invoices often need numbers written in words. This number to words converter spells out values up to the trillions.

Why write numbers in words

Words are harder to alter than digits, so legal and financial documents use both forms. It also aids accessibility for screen readers.

Decimals

Decimal portions are read digit by digit after the word "point", matching common cheque practice.
