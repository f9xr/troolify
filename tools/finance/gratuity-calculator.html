Gratuity Calculator: Estimate Your End-of-Service Benefit

Gratuity is a lump-sum benefit paid by an employer for long service. This gratuity calculator estimates the amount using the widely used statutory formula.

The gratuity formula

For employees covered by the Gratuity Act the formula is last drawn salary x 15 / 26 x years of service, where 15/26 represents fifteen days of wages. For employees not covered, it is typically salary x 15 / 30 x years; this calculator applies the covered formula and rounds service as the Act specifies.

Counting years of service

Under the Act, service beyond six months in a year is rounded up to a full year. The covered option applies that rounding; the non-covered option floors the years.

How to use Gratuity Calculator

  1. Enter your last drawn monthly salary.
  2. Enter completed years of service.
  3. Choose whether you are covered by the Gratuity Act.
  4. Read the estimated gratuity.

Tips for getting accurate results

  • Use your basic plus dearness allowance as the salary figure.
  • Service beyond six months counts as a full year when covered.
  • The statutory cap may limit very large payouts.

Key takeaways

  • Gratuity rewards long service.
  • The formula uses last drawn salary and years served.
  • Covered employees get favourable rounding.

Frequently asked questions

Is the payout capped?

Statutory limits can apply; this tool shows the formula amount before any cap.

Which salary do I enter?

Your last drawn basic salary plus DA.

Do part years count?

They round up under the covered option, otherwise they are floored.

Is my data uploaded?

No, it stays in your browser.
