Discount Calculator: Know What a Sale Price Really Means

Retailers headline percentages, but your budget works in dollars. 50% off can still be a bad deal if the starting price was inflated. This discount calculator shows the percent-off math in the currency that matters - what you actually pay and save.

How Percent-Off Discounts Work

A discount removes a fraction of the original price. For a 25% discount you pay the remaining 75%:

Sale price = original price × (1 - discount%)

The amount saved is original price × discount%. Two discounts always add up to the original price: pay 75%, save 25%.

How to Use the Discount Calculator

  1. Find sale price - enter the original price and the discount percent to see the final price after discount and how much you save.
  2. Find original price - enter the sale price you paid and the discount percent to reveal the pre-discount price and the dollar saving.

Spotting Misleading Discounts

Watch for a fake "original" price inflated before the sale, and remember that a percentage off does not equal profit in your pocket unless it beats the real comparables. Reverse mode is handy here: use the Percentage Calculator to check the percentage change between the marked price and typical prices, and our Tip Calculator to add what you owe on top after shopping.

Stacking Discounts

When a sale coupon applies after a clearance discount, apply them one after another, not by adding the percentages. 20% off then an extra 10% is 28% total off, not 30%. Work it through step by step and check a final price with this calculator before you commit.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate the sale price after a discount?

Multiply the original price by the discount percent and subtract it from the original price: sale price = original × (1 - discount%). For example, 25% off $80 is $80 × 0.75 = $60.

How do I find the original price from the sale price?

Divide the sale price by (1 - discount%). If you paid $60 for an item that was 25% off, the original price was $60 ÷ 0.75 = $80.

Is the Troolify discount calculator safe to use?

Yes. Every calculation runs locally inside your browser tab. Your numbers are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted to a server, so your figures stay completely private.

How do I compare two percent-off deals?

Compare the dollar savings, not just the percentages, because a bigger percent off a small price can be worth less than a smaller percent off a large price. The calculator shows your savings in dollars for each deal.

Conclusion

Sales are only savings if the final price is genuinely low. Run the discount both ways with this tool, compare against typical prices, and you will never overpay for the illusion of a bargain again.
