Percentage Calculator: Every Percentage Question, Answered

Percentages show up in every part of money and daily life: sales, tips, marks, budgets and growth. This calculator handles the three questions people actually ask - a percent of a number, one number as a percent of another, and the change between two numbers - in plain language.

What Is a Percentage?

A percentage is simply a fraction out of 100. "15%" means 15 parts of every 100, or 0.15 when written as a decimal. Because every percentage is a ratio, one single tool can answer percent-of, percent-of-relationship and change questions without any formulas to remember.

How to Use the Percentage Calculator

  1. Pick a mode - percent of a value, what percent of Y is X, or percentage change.
  2. Enter your two numbers. The labels update to match the selected mode.
  3. Read the result in figures and as a full sentence, for example "15% of 200 is 30".

When to Use Each Mode

Use X% of Y to find a part of a whole - like a 18% tip on a bill, or 7% sales tax on a purchase (our Tip Calculator handles split bills with these same percentages). Use what percent of Y is X to compare two numbers, and percentage change to measure growth - combine it with our Discount Calculator when you are comparing marked-down prices.

The Three Formulas, Explained

  • X% of Y: (X × Y) / 100.
  • What percent of Y is X: (X / Y) × 100.
  • Percentage change from X to Y: ((Y - X) / X) × 100 - positive is an increase, negative is a decrease.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate X% of Y?

Multiply X by Y and divide by 100. For example, 15% of 200 is (15 × 200) / 100 = 30. The percent-of mode does this for you instantly.

How do I find what percent one number is of another?

Divide the first number by the second and multiply by 100. For example, 30 is 15% of 200 because (30 ÷ 200) × 100 = 15.

How do I calculate percentage change?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide by the original value, and multiply by 100. A positive result is an increase; a negative result is a decrease.
