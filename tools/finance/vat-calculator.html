VAT Calculator: Add or Remove Tax in Seconds

Value Added Tax (VAT) sits on top of most goods and services across the UK, Europe and beyond. Whether you are pricing products, invoicing a client, or checking a supplier’s quote, you constantly move between net (ex-VAT) and gross (VAT-included) amounts. This free VAT calculator does that maths instantly for any rate.

Adding VAT vs Removing VAT

Add VAT starts from a net price and applies the tax: VAT = net × rate ÷ 100, and the gross total is net + VAT. This is what you do when setting a retail price from a wholesale cost.

Remove VAT starts from a gross price and strips the tax out: net = gross ÷ (1 + rate/100), and the VAT is the difference. This is what you do when whether an invoice shows a VAT-inclusive total and you need the taxable base.

How to Use the VAT Calculator

  1. Choose Add or Remove depending on which figure you have.
  2. Enter the rate - the drop-down suggests common European rates, or type any value from 0 to 100.
  3. Type the amount and read the net, VAT and gross results - they update as you type.

Common VAT Rates Across Europe

The standard rate differs by country: 20% in the UK and France, 19% in Germany, 21% in Spain and the Netherlands, 22% in Italy, and 25% in Denmark and Sweden. Most countries also apply reduced rates to essentials like food and books, plus zero rates in some cases - confirm the applicable rate for your goods before pricing. Pair results with the Percentage Calculator for quick margin checks, or the Discount Calculator when running promotions.

A Note on Rounding

Formal VAT returns usually round to the nearest cent per line or per invoice depending on local rules. This calculator keeps full precision and displays money to two decimal places, which matches everyday invoice practice in the UK and most of the EU.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add VAT to a price?

Choose Add VAT, enter the net (ex-VAT) price and your VAT rate. The VAT amount equals net multiplied by rate divided by 100, and the gross total is net plus VAT.

How do I remove VAT from a total (gross) price?

Choose Remove VAT, enter the gross (VAT-inclusive) price and your VAT rate. The net price is gross divided by (1 + rate/100), and the VAT is the difference between gross and net.

What are the standard VAT rates in different countries?

Common European rates are 20% (UK and France), 19% (Germany), 21% (Spain and the Netherlands), 22% (Italy) and 25% (Denmark and Sweden). Reduced and zero rates exist for specific goods, so always confirm the rate that applies to yours.
