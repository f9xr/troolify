GST Calculator: Add or Extract Goods and Services Tax

Whether you are invoicing or checking a receipt, you often need to move between net and gross prices. This GST calculator adds GST to a net amount or extracts it from a gross one at any rate.

Adding versus removing

Adding GST multiplies the net price by the rate. Removing it divides the gross price by one plus the rate, so you recover the original net figure exactly.

The CGST and SGST split

For intra-state sales the GST is split equally into CGST and SGST; for inter-state sales it becomes a single IGST. The calculator shows the equal split so you can fill invoice lines correctly.

How to use GST Calculator

  1. Enter the amount.
  2. Pick or type the GST rate.
  3. Choose whether the amount is net or gross.
  4. Read the net, GST, gross and the CGST/SGST split.

Tips for getting accurate results

  • Use the Add mode when you know the pre-tax price.
  • Use Remove mode for tax-inclusive prices.
  • Intra-state supplies split into CGST and SGST.

Key takeaways

  • Add multiplies, remove divides.
  • The split depends on the supply type.
  • Any GST rate can be entered.

Frequently asked questions

Which rate should I use?

Use the rate that applies to your goods or services.

Is IGST shown?

The split shown equals CGST plus SGST; IGST is the same total.

Can rates include decimals?

Yes, 0.25% is supported.

Is my data uploaded?

No, it stays in your browser.
