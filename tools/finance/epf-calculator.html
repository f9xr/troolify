EPF Calculator: Project Your Provident Fund Corpus

The Employees Provident Fund is a cornerstone of retirement saving for salaried workers. This EPF calculator projects the balance you could accumulate over your working life.

Both sides contribute

Your employer matches your contribution into the fund. Enter the combined monthly amount for the fullest picture, since both shares accrue interest.

Interest is credited yearly

The fund earns a declared annual interest rate, credited to the balance. Over a long career this compounds into a substantial corpus.

How to use EPF Calculator

  1. Enter your combined monthly EPF contribution.
  2. Set the current EPF interest rate.
  3. Enter your years of service.
  4. Read total contributed, interest and the projected corpus.

Tips for getting accurate results

  • Use the most recent declared interest rate.
  • Include the employer share for accuracy.
  • Long tenures are dominated by interest, not contributions.

Key takeaways

  • Employer contributions boost the corpus.
  • Declared interest compounds over the career.
  • Interest eventually outweighs contributions.

Frequently asked questions

Is the contribution rate fixed?

Statutory rates apply to the basic salary; enter your actual combined amount.

Does it include EPS?

No, this is the provident fund portion only.

Can I model a raise?

This model assumes a level contribution.

Is my data uploaded?

No, it stays local.
