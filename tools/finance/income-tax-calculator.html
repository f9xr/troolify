Income Tax Calculator: A Quick Estimate of What You Owe

Income tax is charged in slabs, so only the income above each threshold is taxed at the higher rate. This income tax calculator applies a progressive slab structure to your income and shows the resulting liability.

How slab tax works

Each slab applies its rate only to the income falling within that band. Your effective rate is therefore always lower than the top rate you reach, which is why the effective rate figure is useful.

Choosing a slab set

Different regimes offer different exemptions in exchange for different rates. Switch the slab set to compare, and remember this tool applies the deduction you enter rather than modelling every exemption.

How to use Income Tax Calculator

  1. Enter your annual income.
  2. Enter a standard deduction if applicable.
  3. Choose a slab set.
  4. Read taxable income, tax, effective rate and net income.

Key takeaways

  • Only income above each threshold is taxed at the higher rate.
  • The effective rate is below the top marginal rate.
  • Deductions reduce the taxable base.

Yes, enter any amount.
