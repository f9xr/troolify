Venmo Fee Calculator: Know Your Exact Costs

Venmo fees vary wildly depending on how you pay and what type of transaction you make. A payment to a friend is free, but Goods & Services, credit cards, instant transfers, Cash a Check, ATM withdrawals and crypto trades all carry different charges. The Troolify Venmo Fee Calculator shows you the exact fee, the net amount you receive and the effective fee percentage for every scenario - computed privately in your browser.

What the Venmo Fee Calculator Does

Enter any amount in USD and the calculator instantly models every major Venmo fee schedule you might hit: Goods & Services at 1.9% + $0.10 (or 2.29% + $0.10 with a credit card), free personal payments with the optional 1.75% instant transfer, Cash a Check at 1.0% (min $5) or 5% faster, the $2.50 ATM withdrawal fee and the 1.8% crypto spread with a $1 minimum.

How to Use the Venmo Fee Calculator

  1. Enter your amount - type any amount in USD, for example 50.00.
  2. Choose a transaction type - Goods & Services, friend payment, Cash a Check, ATM or crypto.
  3. Pick the sub-option - for example credit card vs. bank funding, or faster check disbursement.
  4. Toggle instant transfer - switch it on to see the 1.75% add-on where eligible.
  5. Read the results - fee breakdown, net amount received, effective percentage and a color-coded side-by-side comparison of every applicable fee.

Venmo Fee Breakdown

Present-day Venmo fee structure reproduced by the calculator: Goods & Services payments cost 1.9% plus $0.10, rising to 2.29% plus $0.10 for credit card funding. Personal payments are free when funded by your Venmo balance or a linked bank account, but an instant transfer costs 1.75% of the amount. Cash a Check costs 1.0% with a $5 minimum, or 5.0% for faster funds. Withdrawing from an ATM costs $2.50 per transaction after the first free withdrawal each month, and buying or selling crypto incurs a 1.8% spread with a $1 minimum.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Venmo charge for Goods & Services payments?

Venmo charges 1.9% plus $0.10 per Goods & Services payment when funded by your Venmo balance or a linked bank account. If you pay with a credit card, the fee increases to 2.29% plus $0.10.

How much does a Venmo instant transfer cost?

A Venmo instant transfer to your bank account costs 1.75% of the transferred amount, subject to a minimum of $0.25 and a maximum of $25. Standard transfers to a linked bank account remain free.

Is sending money to a friend on Venmo free?

Yes. Standard personal payments funded by your Venmo balance or a linked bank account are free. Fees only apply when you use a credit card, request an instant transfer, or make a Goods & Services payment.

How accurate is this calculator?

The calculator uses Venmo's published fee schedules and is updated to reflect current pricing. Because fees can change, always double-check the fee shown in the Venmo app before confirming a transaction.
