Mortgage Calculator: Estimate Your Monthly Home Payment

Before you commit to a mortgage, you want to know what the monthly payment will be and how taxes, insurance and fees change it. This mortgage calculator does the full calculation from home price, down payment, rate and term.

What makes up the payment

The principal and interest (P&I) portion follows the standard amortisation formula. On top of that you may pay property tax and insurance, often collected monthly, and any HOA fee. The calculator separates the P&I payment from the total monthly outgoing so you can budget accurately.

Why the down payment matters

A larger down payment reduces the loan amount and therefore the interest you pay across the term. It can also lower your rate with some lenders. Change the down payment field and watch the monthly figure move.

How to use Mortgage Calculator

  1. Enter the home price and your planned down payment.
  2. Set the annual interest rate and the loan term in years.
  3. Optionally add annual tax and insurance and any monthly HOA fee.
  4. Read the monthly principal and interest, total monthly and lifetime interest.

Tips for getting accurate results

  • Enter annual tax and insurance as a single yearly amount.
  • Leave HOA at zero if you are not buying in a managed community.
  • Re-run the numbers at a slightly higher rate as a buffer.

Key takeaways

  • P&I follows the standard amortisation formula.
  • Taxes, insurance and HOA add to the monthly cost.
  • Down payment directly reduces interest over the term.

Frequently asked questions

Does it include PMI?

No. Add private mortgage insurance into the tax and insurance field if your lender requires it.

Can I model a 15-year term?

Yes, change the term field to any number of years.

Is the interest fixed over the term?

The calculator assumes a fixed rate throughout.

Are my numbers stored?

No, everything stays in your browser.

Conclusion

Get the monthly figure nailed down before you make an offer. Then compare borrowing costs with the EMI calculator.
