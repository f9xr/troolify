Savings Goal Calculator: Your Saving Plan, Backwards-Engineered

Goals feel abstract until they have a number and a date. This calculator works backwards from your target: it tells you the exact monthly contribution that gets you there on time, taking your current savings and interest growth into account.

The Math Behind the Monthly Figure

Your goal is reached by a starting balance that grows, plus a stream of monthly deposits that each grow in turn:

Monthly contribution = (goal - current × (1 + i)n) ÷ ((1 + i)n - 1) / i

where i is your monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12) and n is the number of months. The denominator is the future value of a monthly annuity - interest is credited to every deposit, so early contributions work harder than late ones.

How to Use the Savings Goal Calculator

  1. Enter your goal and what you already have saved.
  2. Set a deadline as a number of months or a specific date.
  3. Add an annual interest rate for the account or investment you plan to use (type 0 for a plain cash jar).

The result shows the monthly contribution, total money you will put in, and the interest earned on top.

How Rates and Deadlines Change the Plan

Stretch the deadline and the monthly figure falls - but interest has longer to accumulate, so the total you put in stays lower too. Raising the rate has the same effect: your money works instead of you. Compare a few scenarios before locking a number into your budget.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I save per month to reach a goal?

Divide the shortfall - your goal minus the future value of your current savings - by the future value of a monthly annuity at your interest rate. The calculator does exactly this, factoring in how interest grows both your current balance and each monthly deposit.

How do interest rates affect my savings goal?

A higher rate means your money works harder, so the required monthly contribution is lower. Even small rate differences matter over long horizons, so it is worth comparing accounts before you commit.

Is the Troolify savings goal calculator safe to use?

Yes. Every calculation runs locally inside your browser tab. Your numbers are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted to a server, so your financial figures stay completely private.

What happens if I already have most of the goal saved?

The calculator factors in your current savings. If your existing balance will already grow past the goal thanks to interest, the required monthly contribution drops to zero.

Conclusion

A plan you can actually follow beats a vague ambition. Decide on the target and deadline, run the numbers here, and start with the small monthly amount - it compounds into something real.
