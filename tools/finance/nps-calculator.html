NPS Calculator: Project Your National Pension System Corpus

The National Pension System builds a retirement corpus from regular contributions. This NPS calculator projects that corpus and splits it into the annuity portion and the lump sum you can withdraw.

The annuity rule

Part of the corpus must be used to buy an annuity that pays you a lifelong pension; the remainder can be withdrawn as a lump sum. This calculator lets you set that split.

Compounding over decades

Because NPS horizons are long, the final corpus is dominated by compounding. Even modest monthly contributions grow substantially over thirty years.

How to use NPS Calculator

  1. Enter your monthly contribution.
  2. Set the expected return and years to retirement.
  3. Choose the annuity portion.
  4. Read the total corpus, lump sum and annuity amount.

Tips for getting accurate results

  • Use a realistic long-term return.
  • The annuity minimum is set by regulation, so check current rules.
  • Small monthly increases early make a big difference.

Key takeaways

  • A portion must fund a lifelong annuity.
  • Long horizons magnify compounding.
  • The lump sum is the corpus minus the annuity portion.

Frequently asked questions

Can I withdraw the whole corpus?

No, a minimum portion must buy an annuity under current rules.

Is the return guaranteed?

No, it depends on the chosen schemes.

Can I change contributions?

This model assumes a level monthly contribution.

Is my data uploaded?

No, it stays in your browser.
