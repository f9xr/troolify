FD Interest Calculator: Know Your Fixed Deposit Return

A fixed deposit locks your money at a known rate for a set period. This FD interest calculator shows the interest earned and the maturity value for any principal, rate and tenure.

Compounding frequency matters

Banks often compound deposits quarterly. The more frequently interest compounds, the higher the effective yield, so switch the frequency to match your bank for an accurate figure.

The maturity formula

Maturity is P x (1 + r/m)^(m x t), where r is the annual rate, m the compounding periods per year and t the tenure in years.

How to use FD Interest Calculator

  1. Enter the deposit amount, annual rate and tenure in years.
  2. Select the compounding frequency your bank uses.
  3. Read the interest earned and maturity value.

Frequently asked questions

Does this handle simple interest FDs?

Choose yearly compounding and a one-year tenure for a close approximation.

Is TDS applied?

No, the figure is gross interest before tax.

Can tenures be under a year?

Yes, decimal years are accepted.
