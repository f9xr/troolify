Tip Calculator: Fair Splits, Zero Awkward Math

A dinner out should end with good coffee, not a confusing splitting discussion. This tip calculator turns your bill amount, chosen tip and number of people into clean per-person totals - with tax and without guessing.

How the Tip Math Works

The tip is a percentage of the pre-tax bill: tip = bill × tip%. Tax is then added on top: total = bill + tax + tip. Splitting equally gives each person total ÷ people, and the calculator also shows the per-person share without the tip for cash or pay-your-own-tip situations.

How to Use the Tip Calculator

  1. Enter the bill amount before tax.
  2. Pick a tip percent with the quick buttons, or type your own.
  3. Add tax percent if shown on the receipt (optional).
  4. Enter the number of people - anything from 1 to 100.

How Much Should You Tip?

In the United States, 15-20% of the pre-tax bill is standard, with 18-20% for great service. In many other countries service is included in the bill, so a tip is a gesture, not an expectation. When in doubt, check local custom or ask - and use our Percentage Calculator to check any percentage anywhere.

Splitting the Bill Fairly

Equal splits only work when everyone ordered roughly the same. For variable orders, calculate each person's share separately using this tool, then add the group tip. Keep a small buffer for rounding - splits rarely divide into exact cents, so the calculator rounds for display and you can even the last penny up.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much tip should I leave at a restaurant?

In the United States, 15-20% of the pre-tax bill is the common range, with 18-20% for good service. In many other countries service is included, so check the local custom. The calculator shows the tip in dollars for any percentage you choose.

Should the tip be calculated before or after tax?

Tips are normally based on the pre-tax bill amount. Tax is added after the tip is calculated, and this calculator follows that convention while still showing the fully loaded per-person total.

Is the Troolify tip calculator safe to use?

Yes. Every calculation runs locally inside your browser tab. Your numbers are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted to a server, so your figures stay completely private.

How do I split the bill fairly between people?

Enter the number of people and the calculator divides the total (bill + tax + tip) equally. It also shows the per-person total without the tip, so you can separate the tip share when some people pay cash.

Conclusion

Generosity should never come with anxiety about the math. Run the bill through this calculator, round up the tip if the service deserved it, and walk out knowing exactly what you owe.
