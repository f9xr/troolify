Retirement Calculator: Size the Corpus You Need

Retirement planning starts with two numbers: the corpus you need and the monthly saving that gets you there. This retirement calculator estimates both, adjusting your expenses for inflation.

Building the corpus target

Your current expenses are inflated to the year you retire, then annualised. Dividing by a sustainable withdrawal rate gives the corpus required to fund roughly twenty-five years of retirement at that income.

The monthly saving

Using your pre-retirement return and the years remaining, the calculator works out the monthly contribution that compounds to the corpus target. Start earlier and the requirement drops quickly.

How to use Retirement Calculator

  1. Enter your current age and target retirement age.
  2. Enter your current monthly expenses.
  3. Set inflation and the expected returns before and after retirement.
  4. Read the corpus needed and the monthly saving required.

Tips for getting accurate results

  • Use a conservative post-retirement return.
  • Include major lifestyle costs in your expense figure.
  • Revisit the plan every few years.

Key takeaways

  • Inflation raises the retirement income you need.
  • A sustainable withdrawal rate sets the corpus target.
  • Time in the market reduces the monthly saving.

Frequently asked questions

How many retirement years does it assume?

The corpus funds about twenty-five years of withdrawals.

Does it include pensions?

No, subtract other income from your expenses separately.

Can I model early retirement?

Yes, set a lower retirement age.

Is my data uploaded?

No, it stays in your browser.
