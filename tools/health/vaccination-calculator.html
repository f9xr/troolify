Baby Vaccination Schedule Calculator: Map the Milestones

Immunisation follows an age-based schedule. This baby vaccination schedule calculator turns a birth date into approximate dates for common milestones.

Key ages

Many programmes give initial doses at birth and at six, ten and fourteen weeks, with boosters around nine, twelve and eighteen months. Exact schedules vary by country.

Follow local guidance

This tool shows generic milestone dates. Always follow your national immunisation programme and your pediatrician advice.

How to use Baby Vaccination Schedule Calculator

  1. Enter the baby date of birth.
  2. Review the generated milestone dates.
  3. Cross-check against your local immunisation schedule.
