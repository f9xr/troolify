Cholesterol Ratio Calculator: Read Your Lipid Panel

Cholesterol ratios put your lipid numbers in context. This cholesterol ratio calculator computes several common ratios from a standard panel.

The key ratios

Total-to-HDL and LDL-to-HDL compare atherogenic cholesterol with protective HDL. Triglycerides-to-HDL is a marker associated with insulin resistance.

Reading them with care

Ratios complement, rather than replace, individual values. Your clinician interprets them alongside your overall risk profile.

How to use Cholesterol Ratio Calculator

  1. Enter total cholesterol, HDL and LDL.
  2. Enter triglycerides.
  3. Read the three ratios.

Tips for getting accurate results

  • Use a fasting panel where possible.
  • Ratios improve with exercise and diet.
  • Always interpret with a clinician.

Key takeaways

  • Ratios contextualise lipid values.
  • Higher total-to-HDL generally means higher risk.
  • They support, not replace, clinical judgement.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good total-to-HDL ratio?

Lower is generally better; discuss targets with your clinician.

Do I need to fast?

Fasting improves triglyceride accuracy.

Is this a diagnosis?

No, it is informational.
