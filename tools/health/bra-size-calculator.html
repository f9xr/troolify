Bra Size Calculator: Fit Confidently, Every Time

Most people wear the wrong bra size without ever knowing it - a band that rides up or a cup that wrinkles is a fit problem, not a body problem. This free calculator turns two measurements - your snug underbust and your fullest bust - into a starting size, quickly and privately.

How to Measure for a Bra

  1. Underbust - wrap the tape snugly around your ribcage, just below your breasts. This sets the band.
  2. Bust - measure around the fullest part of your bust, tape parallel to the floor.
  3. Enter both in cm or inches and read your size instantly.

Measure in front of a mirror, breathe normally and use a fabric (not plastic) tape for the most accurate result.

How Band and Cup Are Calculated

Your band comes from the rounded underbust in inches, adjusted to an even number. Your cup is the inch difference between bust and underbust: 1 inch is A, 2 is B, 3 is C and so on. Because cup volume also depends on the band, each size has sister sizes - a 34C and a 32D hold the same cup volume, which is hugely useful when one brand runs tight or loose.

Tips for a Good Fit

The band should feel snug and stay level all day; the cups should hold the whole bust with no overflow and no gaping. Check your size about twice a year, since weight, training and age all shift it. If you are tracking body changes over time, pair this with the Body Fat Percentage Calculator and the BMI Calculator for the full picture.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I measure my bra size correctly?

Measure your underbust snugly around the ribcage just under the breasts, then measure the fullest part of your bust. Enter both into the calculator - the maths for band and cup is done for you.

Why do bra sizes vary between brands?

Brands, countries and styles all fit differently. Sister sizes - the same cup volume one band up or down - are the practical answer, and this tool lists them for your measurement pair.

Is my measurement data shared?

Never. Both measurements are processed entirely in your browser tab and nothing is uploaded or stored - that is why the result appears instantly.

When should I re-check my bra size?

Re-measure around twice a year, or whenever a band rides up, cups overflow, or a previously comfortable bra stops fitting.

Conclusion

Getting your measurements right is the whole battle. Use this calculator as your baseline, confirm with an in-person fitting when you can, and let sister sizes do the rest.
