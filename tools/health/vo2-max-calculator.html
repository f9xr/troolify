VO2 Max Calculator: Measure Your Aerobic Capacity

VO2 max is the maximum volume of oxygen your body can use during intense exercise, and it is one of the best single measures of cardiovascular fitness. This VO2 max calculator estimates it two ways.

Two estimation methods

The heart-rate method uses the ratio of maximum to resting heart rate, known as the Uth-Sorensen-Overgaard-Pedersen estimate. The Cooper method uses the distance covered in a twelve-minute run.

What the number means

Higher values indicate better aerobic fitness. Typical ranges differ by age and sex, and training, particularly interval work, can raise VO2 max over time.

How to use VO2 Max Calculator

  1. Choose the heart-rate or Cooper method.
  2. Enter the required values.
  3. Read your estimated VO2 max and rating.

Tips for getting accurate results

  • Measure resting heart rate on waking.
  • Estimate maximum heart rate as 220 minus age if untested.
  • Keep conditions consistent between tests.

Key takeaways

  • VO2 max measures aerobic capacity.
  • Two field methods estimate it without a lab.
  • Higher is better; training can improve it.

Frequently asked questions

Which method is more accurate?

Lab testing is most accurate; both field methods are estimates.

What is a good VO2 max?

It varies by age and sex; above 42 is generally strong.

Can I improve it?

Yes, with consistent aerobic and interval training.

Is my data uploaded?

No, it stays local.
