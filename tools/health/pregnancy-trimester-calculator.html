Pregnancy Trimester Calculator: Track Your Stage

Pregnancy is divided into three trimesters. This pregnancy trimester calculator shows your current week and trimester from your last period date.

How dating works

Pregnancy is dated from the first day of your last menstrual period, with an estimated due date about forty weeks later. This simple method edges slightly ahead of true conception dating.

The three trimesters

The first runs to about week twelve, the second to about week twenty-seven, and the third to birth. Each has its own milestones and care schedule.

How to use Pregnancy Trimester Calculator

  1. Enter the first day of your last period.
  2. Read your current week, trimester and estimated due date.
