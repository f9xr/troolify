Weight Loss Calculator: How Long Will It Take?

Weight loss comes down to a calorie deficit sustained over time. This weight loss calculator estimates how long a given daily deficit takes you to reach your goal weight.

The energy maths

Roughly 7,700 calories make up a kilogram of body weight. A daily deficit of 500 calories, for example, produces about half a kilogram a week.

Keeping it healthy

Very large deficits are hard to sustain and can harm health. Aim for steady loss and consult a professional for a plan tailored to you.

How to use Weight Loss Calculator

  1. Enter your current and goal weight.
  2. Enter your planned daily calorie deficit.
  3. Read the weight to lose, weekly rate and estimated time.

Tips for getting accurate results

  • A deficit of 300 to 500 calories is usually sustainable.
  • Recalculate as your weight changes.
  • Combine diet with resistance training to preserve muscle.

Key takeaways

  • Deficit times time equals weight lost.
  • Half a kilogram a week needs about a 500-calorie deficit.
  • Slow, steady loss is easier to keep off.
