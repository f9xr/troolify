BMI Calculator: Understand Your Body Mass Index in Seconds

Body Mass Index (BMI) is one of the most widely used starting points for understanding whether your weight is in a healthy range for your height. It is a single number that screens for weight-related health risk, and with this free tool you can calculate it instantly without entering any personal data on a server.

What Is BMI and What Does It Tell You?

BMI is your weight in kilograms divided by your height in meters squared (kg/m²). It estimates body fat for most adults and is used by the World Health Organization to define weight categories.

  • Below 18.5 - Underweight
  • 18.5 to 24.9 - Normal (healthy) range
  • 25 to 29.9 - Overweight
  • 30 or above - Obese

Because BMI does not measure body fat directly, it is best paired with other indicators such as waist circumference and physical activity levels.

How to Use the BMI Calculator

  1. Choose your units - switch between metric (cm/kg) and imperial (ft/in/lb).
  2. Enter your height and weight using the fields provided.
  3. Read your results immediately - your BMI value, category badge and healthy weight range appear live.

Your Healthy Weight Range

A healthy weight is any weight that puts your BMI between 18.5 and 24.9. For your height, the calculator computes the exact lower and upper bounds in kilograms (and pounds if you prefer), so you always know how much room you have in either direction. This is often harder to estimate mentally than the BMI itself - the tool does the math for the whole healthy span for you.

Why BMI Matters, and Where It Falls Short

Tracking BMI is a convenient way to spot trends over time, which is why it pairs well with the Ideal Weight Calculator when setting goals. For daily energy planning, combine it with the BMR Calculator (to learn how many calories your body burns at rest) and the Calorie Calculator (to estimate daily needs for weight control).

Remember: BMI can overestimate body fat for very muscular people and underestimate it for older adults with lower muscle mass. Treat it as a screening signal, not a verdict, and discuss any concerns with a healthcare professional.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy BMI range?

A BMI of 18.5 to 24.9 kg/m² is considered the healthy (normal) range for most adults by the World Health Organization. Below 18.5 is underweight, 25 to 29.9 is overweight, and 30 or above is obese.

Why does BMI matter for health?

BMI is a quick screening indicator of body fat in most adults. Outside the healthy range, the risk of conditions such as type 2 diabetes, cardiovascular disease and joint problems tends to rise. It is a useful starting point, but it does not measure body fat directly.

Is BMI always accurate?

No. BMI is a screening tool, not a diagnosis. It can overestimate body fat in very muscular people and underestimate it in older adults who have lost muscle mass. Waist measurement, body-fat testing and clinical assessment add useful context.

What is a healthy weight for my height?

A healthy weight keeps your BMI between 18.5 and 24.9. Multiply 18.5 and 24.9 by your height in meters squared to get your exact range - this calculator does that automatically for any height you enter.

Conclusion

BMI is a fast, free way to understand where your weight sits relative to your height. Use this calculator to get your number, your category and your healthy weight range, then combine it with other health tools to build a clearer picture of your goals.
