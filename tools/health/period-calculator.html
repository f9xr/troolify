Period Calculator: Predict Your Next Cycle

Knowing roughly when your next period and fertile window fall helps with planning and awareness. This period calculator projects your next six cycles from your last period and average cycle length.

How the prediction works

Each cycle is assumed to be the same length. Adding the cycle length repeatedly projects future period start dates, and ovulation is estimated about fourteen days before the next period.

Cycles vary

Real cycles fluctuate with stress, travel and health. Use the predictions as a guide, not a certainty, and adjust the average as you learn your pattern.

How to use Period Calculator

  1. Enter the first day of your last period.
  2. Enter your average cycle and period length.
  3. Read the predicted dates and ovulation estimates.

Tips for getting accurate results

  • Use your typical cycle length, not a single month.
  • Ovulation estimates are rough.
  • Irregular cycles need medical advice.

Key takeaways

  • Predictions assume a regular cycle.
  • Ovulation is estimated before the next period.
  • Actual cycles can vary.
