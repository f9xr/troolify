A1C to Blood Sugar Calculator: Estimated Average Glucose

HbA1c reflects average blood sugar over the previous two to three months. This A1C to blood sugar calculator converts it to estimated average glucose.

The ADAG equation

The relationship is eAG (mg/dL) = 28.7 x A1C - 46.7. Dividing by about eighteen converts the result to millimoles per litre.

Using the figure

Estimated average glucose helps connect a lab A1C with day-to-day readings, though individual variation means meter readings may not match exactly.
