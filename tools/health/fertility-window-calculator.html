Fertility Window Calculator: Identify Your Most Fertile Days

Conception is most likely in the few days around ovulation. This fertility window calculator estimates that window from your cycle length and last period date.

The fertile window

Sperm can survive several days, so the fertile window starts about five days before ovulation and ends about a day after. The days just before ovulation are the most fertile.

Estimates, not guarantees

Ovulation timing varies between cycles. This tool is educational and should not be used as a contraceptive method.

How to use Fertility Window Calculator

  1. Enter the first day of your last period.
  2. Enter your average cycle length.
  3. Read your estimated ovulation and fertile window.
