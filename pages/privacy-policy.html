Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Troolify ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our website and free online tools at troolify.

Last updated: August 24, 2026

1. Overview

Troolify is built around one core principle: your data never leaves your device. Every tool on this site - from the Word Counter to the Password Generator - runs entirely inside your web browser using client-side code. The text, numbers, files or other input you enter into our tools is processed locally on your device and is never uploaded, transmitted, stored or seen by us.

2. Information we collect

Tool inputs. Nothing. Because all processing happens in your browser, we receive no copies of the content you work with.

Automatically collected technical data. Like most websites, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, referring page and request time. This logging is performed by GitHub under its own privacy practices (GitHub Privacy Statement) and is used only to deliver and secure the site.

Communications you send us. If you email us or submit feedback, we receive whatever information you choose to share (such as your email address and message contents). We use it solely to respond to you and improve the site.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

Troolify itself does not set tracking cookies. However, third parties that serve content or advertising on the site may set cookies or use similar technologies as described below.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.
  • We may display advertising from Google AdSense or other networks. These partners operate under their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

5. Third-party services

The site loads some resources from third parties strictly to function: fonts (Google Fonts), icons (Font Awesome via CDN), hosting (GitHub Pages), launch badges from partner platforms, and outbound links to our parent site f9xr.github.io. When you click external links you leave Troolify; we are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those destinations.

6. Data retention

Because we do not collect tool inputs, there is nothing for us to retain from your usage. Email correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle your request, after which it may be deleted.

7. Children's privacy

Troolify is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since our tools do not transmit any personal data to us, children can use them without providing any information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Your rights

EEA / UK (GDPR). Where the GDPR applies, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and to data portability. Because our tools process no personal data server-side, these rights mainly relate to correspondence you send us. You may also withdraw consent to personalized advertising through the opt-out links in Section 4.

California (CCPA/CPRA). We do not sell or share personal information as defined by California law. California residents may request disclosure of, or deletion of, any personal information we hold by contacting us.

9. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Because sensitive operations stay on your device, the most common risk of cloud-based tools - transmitting confidential content over the network - does not apply to Troolify.
