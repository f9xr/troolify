ACCESSIBILITY · TROOLIFY

Accessibility Statement

Troolify is committed to making our tools usable by everyone, regardless of ability.

Our commitment

We aim to make Troolify accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, motor, auditory, and cognitive impairments. We continually work toward conforming to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

What we do

  • Keyboard navigation — every interactive element is reachable and operable with a keyboard.
  • Screen reader support — semantic HTML and descriptive labels for forms and tools.
  • Contrast and legibility — color schemes are chosen to meet contrast requirements.
  • Text alternatives — images and icons include descriptive alternative text.
  • Responsive design — pages adapt to different screen sizes and zoom levels.

Feedback

We are always working to improve accessibility. If you encounter a barrier while using Troolify, please email tontufytservices@gmail.com or use our Feedback page. Please describe the issue and the page or tool where you experienced it, and we will respond within 2 business days.
