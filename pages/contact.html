Common questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

How quickly will you reply to my email?

We usually reply within 1-2 business days. For urgent bug reports, the Feedback page is often the fastest route.

Is Troolify really free and private?

Yes. Every Troolify tool is free, requires no sign up, and runs 100% client-side in your browser. Your data never leaves your device.

Can I suggest a new tool or report a bug?

Absolutely. Use our Feedback page and the F9XR Development Team will review every submission. Popular suggestions are added to our roadmap.

Can the F9XR Team build or redesign my website?

Yes. F9XR works with startups, local businesses, and growing companies on website development, redesign, local SEO, and digital presence strategy. Visit f9xr.github.io to get started.

I have a media or partnership inquiry. Who do I contact?

Email us at tontufytservices@gmail.com with the subject line starting with “Media” or “Partnership” so we can route it to the right person quickly.
