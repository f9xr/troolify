About Troolify

A growing collection of free online tools, built to run entirely inside your browser.

What is Troolify?

Troolify is a privacy-first workspace on the internet where everyday digital tasks are handled instantly, securely, and completely inside your browser. Instead of jumping between dozens of websites, you get one organized hub of practical utilities.

Every tool on Troolify runs 100% client-side. Your text, files, and data never leave your device, which makes the platform a safer option for developers, marketers, students, and business owners who handle sensitive information.

Our mission

We believe useful software should be fast, simple, and respectful of your privacy. No accounts, no uploads, no tracking, and no unnecessary complexity. Just tools that get the job done and get out of your way.

What makes Troolify different?

  • Privacy by default - all processing happens in your browser tab, never on a server.
  • Free forever - no sign-ups, no paywalls, no hidden costs.
  • Fast and lightweight - no bloated interfaces or waiting for reloads.
  • Always growing - new tools are added regularly by the F9XR Team.
